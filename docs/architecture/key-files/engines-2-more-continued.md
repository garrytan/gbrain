- `src/shadow/` + `src/commands/engine-shadow.ts` — Rust engine shadow mode: sampled reads compared in the background, plus a seeded Rust store that follows the brain's writes (ENGINES.md "Rust engine shadow mode"). `shadow/state.ts` (`shadowStateFor(config)`) reads `configDir()/shadow/<brain>/state.json` (brain key `<engine>-<12 hex>` from `brainIdentity`) and returns null when shadow mode is off, the file is missing or unreadable, or `GBRAIN_SHADOW_CHILD=1`; `engine-factory.ts` calls it and, only when it is on, dynamically imports `shadow/attach.ts`, which wraps the engine (`shadow/wrapper.ts`) so a sampled read or search (`shadow/reads.ts`) is answered by the primary first and then offered to a bounded fail-open `MirrorQueue` (`shadow/mirror.ts`: sheds on overflow, never awaits the transport) as a capture function the queue runs synchronously when it has room, before the caller sees the result; the arguments are encoded (`shadow/codec.ts`, functions and signals dropped) before the primary call and the result inside the capture, so later changes by the caller cannot cause a false diff; a sampled read encodes its arguments even when the queue then sheds it, and only an accepted read also encodes its result; calls inside a transaction are skipped. `shadow/supervisor.ts` (`ShadowSupervisor`) spawns `gbrain engine shadow __child <dir>` lazily through `src/core/spawn.ts`, unref'd, with stderr to `child.log`, restarts it with doubling backoff, disables mirroring after 5 exits in 10 minutes (`restart_budget_spent`), kills a child silent for 60 s, refuses lines past an 8 MiB stdin buffer (write samples are held to 8 MiB still in the pipe, watched through their write callbacks because Bun's `writableLength` reads 0), write sample lines are staged and one `setImmediate` flush per event-loop turn writes `write-owed.<session>.json` (everything accepted for the child, and `owed_qualifying`, the page and link writes among it by `sampleQualifyingWeight`, flushed file and directory, never inside the caller's request) before it hands the staged lines to the pipe in their original order; plain weight-1 samples share one record: the first writes a `v:2` open marker (`open_until`, through the `writeOwed` seam) and the record stating the whole count (`v:1`) is written at the close (`OWED_WINDOW_MS` 100 ms, `OWED_WINDOW_SAMPLES` 256, `drain`, `stop`, exit, or any known-loss line or `owe`; option `owedWindowMs` 0 keeps the per-turn flush), about two durable writes (four fsyncs) per save, where the per-turn flush paid one per turn; a marker whose write failed refuses its sample and may stand, so `markerStray` makes the next flush (or a `retryMs` timer, `armStrayRepair`) replace it with a record of what was accepted, even with nothing staged, and a failed close keeps the lines staged and retries; `closing` freezing admission while a close is in doubt (a weighted line is refused as `send_shed`, `owe` raises the count and publishes at once), and a `v:2` record at an orphan pass books `missing - 1` as `receipt_orphaned` and one `write_window_lost` (`write_windows_lost` in status, criterion 5 no evidence; `WriteTally.missing(n, windowLost)`, never bucketed into `other`; the locked remover `removeSettledOwed` (its `tombEpoch` argument) leaves the settled marker as a `v: 2` tombstone with `booked_epoch` instead of deleting it, counted by `readWriteSnapshot` as `windowFiles` and kept until its epoch is over, when a pass of a later epoch removes it (`removeStaleTomb`, under the same tally lock, on the record read again there: it deletes only a tombstone of an earlier epoch, and a republished, higher record stays; `removeOwed` is the bare deletion both removers call, and it is never called outside the lock)); a failed close is a failed owed write, so `keep` and the write-pending marker below apply to it; a failed write keeps them staged and retries, and a stage bound counts lines of every kind. At disconnect `drain` waits up to 2 s for the pipe to flush, and `closeOwed` closes the record (`closed`; `child_exited` is written when the child dies) so the child books any sample it never read as `pipe_unflushed` (capability_missing `pipe_unflushed_nonqualifying` for one that was not a page or link write; an open marker's one extra sample counts as a page and link write, and is the one booked as `write_window_lost`). `shadow/write-compare.ts` keeps the other half: `WriteTally.note` takes the credit a verdict settles and saves it in the same locked rename (`settled[session]` and its page-and-link part `settled_qualifying[session]`, carried into a new epoch together with `lost_applied` and `applied`), a client or retired child writes `no_epoch`/`epoch_retired` lost lines that name the session (numbered per writer: a batch retried after a partial or unflushed append writes the same numbers, and the owner takes each once, in the tally's `line_seq`), `readWriteSnapshot` reads tally, then unfolded lost lines, then owed files, then the tally's identity again so status can only over-block, and `WriteTally.obligations` (at start, each save and every 10 s) books a gone session's `owed - settled` once as `receipt_orphaned`, split by `owed_qualifying` less `settled_qualifying` (the rest is capability_missing `receipt_orphaned_nonqualifying`; `LOST_NONQUALIFYING` folds those lines), and removes its file only after a tally read back from disk covers it; the child removes its own closed file at the end of its input under the same rule, and stamps an open `v: 2` marker it settled with its own epoch (a tombstone) instead of removing it. Legacy `write-sent.*` receipts, lost lines with no session (`legacy_lines`) and `sent_count n=`/`no_epoch n=` faults from older writers are still read as no evidence. Swap-readiness evidence: `shadow/evidence-epoch.ts` keeps `evidence/epoch.json` (seed id, addon SHA-256, comparator and adapter versions, capability-set hash, readiness-policy hash; a changed input opens a new epoch, an unrelated release does not) and the child's content-free `rust_internal_error` fault recorder; ledger rows, usage lines and faults carry the epoch id. `shadow/exactness.ts` is the one table of comparisons that must match exactly (every `exact` read, `findByTitleFuzzy`, and the `ts_rank` keyword and title searches; `searchTakes` and `searchTakesVector` count only a different row set, and `searchVector` only a diff under `excludePrivate` whose extra Rust rows did not all pass the per-row privacy check); a mismatch there is `stale` only when the child's follower held older committed, unapplied work, else `inconclusive` and gets a stable rerun. `shadow/usage.ts` and the `UsageMeter` in `mirror.ts` count every call per operation class (offered, sampled out, shed, in a transaction, unmirrorable) into `usage/<day>.jsonl`. `shadow/evidence.ts` `buildEvidenceStatus` (spread into `shadowStatusDocument`) folds the current epoch's ledger, usage files and whole fault history into `reads`, `privacy_diverged`, `search.trigram_exact_disagreements`, `search.keyword_title`, `search.vector_recall_at_10`, `search.primary_recall_at_10` and `child` (absent, never zero, when evidence is missing: `privacy_diverged` also while a privacy check of the last 7 days is unsettled, the recall fields until 30 samples are scored) and prints the epoch start, the earliest READY date and the 100-per-class engineering default. Epoch opening is serialized across processes by `evidence/epoch.lock`; the epoch names the addon the child loaded, so a child that is only a client of another process's owner (whose addon digest the owner handshake does not carry) opens none and its rows count for nothing, until it takes over. `SHADOW_ADAPTER_VERSION` (pinned to a digest of the adapter source by a test) is an epoch input. Serving processes append each operation class's first use per epoch at once (`usage/`), and the follower's probe, fingerprint totals, allocator result, lag histograms and reconcile passes are tagged with the evidence epoch and reported only for the current one. An unparsable or malformed fault line makes the child fields absent. `follower.json` names its seed in `seed_id`; the status `epoch_id` and `epoch_started_at` come from the manifest. A text-ordered read (`getTags`, `listPages` with `sort: 'slug'`) whose rows are the primary's re-sorted in C byte order is `collation_order`: explained and counted as successful coverage, with its own count per class in `evidence.collation_order`; never an unexplained divergence.
