- `src/core/engine-sql/facts-conversation.ts` — the statements of conversation fact publication and relink as 30 typed engine members in place of `executeRaw`, each taking `raw: EngineRawRead` first. `facts/conversation-publication.ts` reads and writes a page's conversation facts through them (newest fact id, active and window counts, held slots, the five-step clear that `clearConversationPageFacts` runs, outcome-row delete, purge-tombstone ordinals, source-window moves and stamps, the write journal reads), `relink-publish.ts`, `conversation-relink.ts`, `conversation-relink-carry.ts`, `conversation-entity-hints.ts`, `conversation-memo.ts`, `conversation-incremental.ts` and `relink.ts` read relinked, unbacked and unlinked rows, hint pages, the memo outcome row and the page lock. It exports `RELINKED_DERIVED_ROW` and `LABEL_RETIRED_MARKER`; the caller modules re-export them. Both engines delegate each member; the Rust engine refuses them by name with `engine_capability_missing` (capability `facts`). Tests: `test/facts-conversation-sites-sql-pin.test.ts` (statement text and binds against the pre-change text in `test/helpers/facts-conversation-base-sql.ts`) and `test/engine-parity/facts-conversation-sites.test.ts` (21 parity cases).

Pinned by `test/dream-cycle-sites-sql-pin.test.ts` and `test/engine-parity/dream-cycle-sites.test.ts`; the Rust engine refuses each member by name.

- `src/core/rust-engine/snapshot-overlay.ts` + `rust/crates/gbrain-store/src/page_withdrawals.rs` — the withdrawal overlay of a Rust page snapshot (`readPageSnapshot`, `getPage`, `readPageSnapshotsBatch`, the timeline `sealChunks` seals): the store answers the page's withdrawal and purge ledger rows, and the member strikes withdrawn fence rows and drops purged ones as `page-state/snapshot.ts` does, so a forgotten fact leaves Rust page reads and rebuilt chunks. - `src/core/persistence/capability-retry.ts` — the retry limit of a write an engine refuses at publication with its typed capability refusal (`engine_capability_missing`, a member the Rust engine does not serve yet). `coordinator.ts` `finishUnpublishedFailure` charges each such refusal with `chargeCapabilityRefusal(requestId)`: under `PUBLICATION_CAPABILITY_ATTEMPTS` (3) claims it returns the claim to queued, so a consumer on an engine that serves the member can still publish it; on the last claim the write ends `failed` with the refusal, its stored detail adds `attempts` and `limit`, and the waiter's error (`service.ts` `writeResponse`) carries the capability as `reason` and the member in `detail`, so it never reads as `write_pending` and the writes behind it in its source go on. The count lives in the consumer process (bounded map, no column) and is dropped once the write ends in any terminal state (`settleCapabilityRefusal`, from `consumer.ts` `settled` and after the failed completion commits); a restarted consumer counts again from zero. A refusal in preparation ends failed on its first attempt. Pinned by `test/engine-parity/publication-retry-limit.test.ts` (an injected refusal on all three engines, and put_page over MCP on stdio and http) and `test/engine-parity/bench-save-repro.test.ts` (the real Rust refusals).

The SQL text is the text `persistence/deactivation.ts`, `activation.ts`, `pre-activation-release.ts`, `admin-lock.ts`, `blocking-effects.ts`, `legacy-locks.ts`, `topology-receipts.ts`, `topology-locks.ts` and `managed-mode.ts` ran through `executeRaw`; both engines delegate in one line each. The Rust engine serves them in `src/core/rust-engine/store-members/writer-release.ts` from the store's brain-mode record and worktree rows (`rust/docs/store/persistence.md`, "Writer release"); `listBlockingEffects`, `readWriterAdminLock` and `assertWriterAdminUnlocked` stay on raw SQL. Pinned by `test/engine-parity/writer-release.test.ts` (CLI deactivate and activate on every arm), `rust/crates/gbrain-store/tests/writer_release.rs` and `test/persistence-mode-fail-closed-6482.test.ts`.
