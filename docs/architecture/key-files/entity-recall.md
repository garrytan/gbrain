# Key files — entity recall (mention index)

The mention index links every page that names an entity (by title, title
subject, frontmatter alias or declared code) to that entity page, keeps the
links current from `gbrain extract --stale`, and serves them on the `entity`
card and `get_backlinks`. Guide: [entity recall](../../guides/entity-recall.md).

- `src/core/by-mention.ts` — the pure mention matcher plus its gazetteer. `buildGazetteer(engine, opts)` loads linkable entity titles and aliases per source (drops below-minimum, generic, ambiguous-first-word and colliding names (a name claimed at one origin by an identity-sibling group stays, marked `shared`), reported as `DroppedName`), keyed by lowercase first token and sorted longest-first. `findMentionedEntities(text, gazetteer, opts)` masks code with `stripCodeBlocks`, then picks the longest matching entry at each token (maximal munch), first mention per target only (a `shared` match links every sibling with those tokens, unless the scanning page is one of them), skipping self-links and other sources unless `allowCrossSource` (an own-source twin wins). Word-run scripts match whole tokens; Han and Kana match character substrings. A Hangul name must start a word, keep its written spacing, and end at a non-Hangul character or an attached title/particle/copula suffix (`HANGUL_NAME_SUFFIX_RE`), so 지원에게 matches and 지원하는 does not ([measurement](../../designs/hangul-mention-boundaries.md)). A matcher change bumps `MENTION_EXTRACTOR_VERSION` (`mentions/pass.ts`, every page rescans once) and the semantics tag inside `hashGazetteer` (the `extract links --by-mention` resume checkpoint). `scanStaleMentions` is the read-only doctor scan for mention rows the current gazetteer no longer produces.
- `src/core/mentions/policy.ts` — the one linkable-type resolver. `linkableTypesFor(pack, policy)`: `person`, `company`, `organization`, `entity` (`ALWAYS_LINKABLE_TYPES`, never removable) plus every pack type with `primitive: entity` and its type aliases (`product` excluded), adjusted by `mentions.entity_types` (`+type`/`type` adds, `-type` removes). `readMentionPolicy` reads `auto_link`, `mentions.auto_link`, `mentions.entity_types`, `mentions.ignore`, `mentions.exclude_slugs` (#5829: listed pages get no gazetteer entry by title or alias, dropped as `excluded_slug`; JSON array or comma list, `parseNameList`), `mentions.alias_deny`, `mentions.multiword_aliases` and `mentions.sibling_merge` (both default on). `canonicalTypeOf` / `typeAliasPairs` map a stored type through the pack's type aliases (`untyped` for none); pairs are bound as two `text[]`. `loadSourcePack(engine, sourceId, {strict})` returns null on a resolution failure unless strict. Tests: `test/mentions-policy-aliases.test.ts`.
- `src/core/mentions/siblings.ts` — identity siblings: pages in one source naming the same normalized title subject under pairwise different prefixes, all linkable entities of one canonical type other than `person`, none excluded or `identity: separate`, at most `SIBLING_GROUP_CAP` (3; a larger group is `capped`). `siblingVerdict` is the one rule (card, search fan-out, gazetteer); `readIdentitySiblings` reads a page's group with the private-page predicate for untrusted callers. Nothing is written to `entity_identities` and nothing is unioned. Tests: `test/mentions-identity-siblings.test.ts`.
- `src/core/mentions/aliases.ts` — derived names of an entity page, implementing `docs/designs/ALIAS_CONVENTIONS.md`. `titleSubject` (text after the last `: `), `declarationsIn` (each declaration with its line: prose cues, label and value lines and table rows whose label names an alias field, a quoted defined term right after the page's own name; a capture is a quoted name or a run of up to 4 capitalized or code tokens ending at punctuation, a lowercase word or the line end, rejected on a possessive, a run of only common words or a cue after a relational noun; linear in the line length), `declaredNames` (its post-filtered names, shared with search's `aliasDeclarations`), `deriveEntityAliases(page, {multiword, deny})` (subject `origin='subject'`, declarations `origin='declared'` with their lines, read from `publicBody`: private takes and facts fences stripped; a single-token declaration is `case_sensitive`; `mentions.alias_deny` and frontmatter `alias_deny` reject as `denied`). `aliasRejection`: under 4 characters, a generic word, or the first word of the page's own multi-word name. Rejections are returned with their line for explain. Tests: `test/mentions-policy-aliases.test.ts`.
- `src/core/mentions/pass.ts` — `runMentionPass(engine, {sourceId?, deadline?, signal?, beforePublish?})`, run by `extractStaleFromDB` (unmanaged) and `extractManagedStaleLinks` (managed, unless `mentions: false`). Per source: policy off → `disableSource` deletes plain mention links (`link_kind` null or `plain`; `typed_ner` stays), derived alias rows, saved entries and page state once and records `disabled`; a changed policy fingerprint (types, cross-source flag, ignore list, `mentions.alias_deny`, `mentions.multiword_aliases`, `mentions.sibling_merge`) marks every page due and recounts `pending`; the status row's `policy_fingerprint` carries the extractor versions its count reflects after the hash (`<hash>;v<mention>.<alias>`, `INDEX_VERSION_STAMP`), and a stamp that differs from the binary's recounts `pending` once (version-behind pages are already due, so nothing is invalidated); `refreshAliases` rewrites derived rows of entity pages whose `page_mention_state.alias_revision`/`alias_version` differ (revision-checked, coordinated write when managed) and removes them from pages that stopped being entities; `buildGazetteer({strict: true})` failing writes nothing and records `failed`; `saveEntries` diffs `mention_gazetteer_entries` under `pg_advisory_xact_lock`, bumps the source generation and marks due only pages linking to a removed target plus pages a mini-gazetteer of added names matches (ASCII names prefiltered with `LIKE` on lower-cased text, others scanned in JS; a first save marks the source due); `reconcileSource` scans due pages (no state row, or `mention_revision`/`mention_version` differ) in batches of 200 and publishes only when the source generations (`FOR SHARE`) and each page's `knowledge_revision` (`FOR SHARE`) are unchanged, writing link diffs (`addLinksBatch`, audit site `extract.mentions`) and page state in one transaction; then the status row gets state, `pending` (`countIndexDuePages`: mention-due pages plus linkable entity pages whose derived aliases are behind their content or `ALIAS_DERIVATION_VERSION`, counted when the source finishes or the deadline stops it) and `last_pass_at`. `writeDerivedAliases` / `writePageAliases` are import's and `reindex --aliases`' alias projection. `MENTION_EXTRACTOR_VERSION` and `ALIAS_DERIVATION_VERSION` are independent of `LINK_EXTRACTOR_VERSION_TS`. The scanned text is title, compiled truth and timeline; frontmatter `mention_ignore` names are skipped per page. Tests: `test/mentions-pass.test.ts`, `test/mentions-coverage-version.test.ts`, `test/mentions-managed.test.ts`, `test/e2e/mentions-parity.test.ts`.
- `src/core/mentions/stale.ts` — the pass as a sweep phase: `previewMentionPass` (index-due pages per source, as `countIndexDuePages` counts them, and newest `last_pass_at`, for `--dry-run`, doctor `links_extraction_lag` and post-upgrade), `linkPhaseDeadline` (a budgeted sweep gives link work half its budget while mention-due pages exist), `formatMentionSummary`, `mentionJsonFields`.
- `src/core/mentions/coverage.ts` — `readMentionCoverage(engine, sourceIds)`: `{state, pending_pages, last_pass_at, degraded?}` from `mention_index_status` plus due pages written at or after the row's `counted_at` (states `complete`, `pending`, `disabled`, `failed`; the card adds `type_not_linkable`). A row whose version stamp differs from the binary's (counted by another release) is recounted once with `countIndexDuePages` and the count and stamp saved on the row (compare-and-set on the stored fingerprint; a failed write still returns the count), so after a version bump every page behind `MENTION_EXTRACTOR_VERSION` or `ALIAS_DERIVATION_VERSION` reads as pending whatever its `updated_at`, and later reads stay one status read plus the recent-pages query. Policy settings changes are detected by the pass, not here. `mentionCoverageNotice` builds the `mention_index` notice (kind `degraded`, a per-call code): the free catch-up sweep for pending/failed, the user's setting for disabled/type-not-linkable, each with a read-only verify.
- `src/core/mentions/referrers.ts` — the shared referrer query behind `referenced_by` and `get_backlinks {group:"page"}`: one row per referring page (every link source), semi-join over links to the target (plus identity co-members under `entity_identity.union`), canonical type via bound alias arrays, date `COALESCE(effective_date, updated_at)` with `date_source`, previews (160 characters after an optional `# ` heading, fences stripped, `redactFindings`). `readReferrerGroups`: per-type totals and a bounded top-10 per type, water-filled to 50 rows per card. `readReferrerPage`: keyset paging on `(date DESC, source_id, slug)` with an opaque cursor (microsecond UTC text). Untrusted scopes apply `privatePagesFilterFragment` to referrer and target and `privateLinkOriginFilterFragment` to the link, in rows and counts. Tests: `test/mentions-card-backlinks.test.ts`, `test/entity-card-perf.slow.test.ts` (10,000-link hub gate).
- `src/core/mentions/upgrade-notice.ts` — `gbrain post-upgrade` prints an `[AGENT]` block naming `gbrain extract --stale --catch-up` with a time estimate (`MENTION_PASS_PAGES_PER_SECOND`) while index-due pages exist (`previewMentionPass`), including every page behind the binary's mention or alias version on an unchanged brain. Post-upgrade does not run the sweep; autopilot's cycle extract phase continues it on its stale-drain budget (`CYCLE_STALE_DRAIN_BUDGET_MS`).
- `src/core/ops/backlinks-paged.ts` — `get_backlinks`' `type` / `group` / `limit` / `cursor`. With none, `ops/links.ts` returns the legacy `Link[]`. `group: "page"` → `{rows, total, truncated, cursor, coverage}` (target = the first source in scope holding the slug; referrers from the read scope). Array mode filters by referrer type and cuts at `limit` with a `listing_truncated` notice naming the paged call. `limit` outside 1..500, a foreign cursor, or an unknown type (not a pack type, alias, observed referrer type or `untyped`) is `invalid_params` naming the valid values.
- `src/commands/extract-mentions-explain.ts` — `gbrain extract mentions --explain <name|slug> [--page <slug>] [--source-id <id>] [--json]` (dispatched from `extract-explain.ts`): the matched entries with origin and case flag, or one reason code (`ambiguous_first_word`, `below_min_length`, `generic_token`, `alias_collision`, `case_mismatch`, `type_not_linkable`, `linking_disabled`, `pending`, `ignored_by_page`, `ignored_by_config`, `denied_by_config`, `not_a_known_name`); only `pending` names a sweep. A slug query also lists each derived name with origin and declaring line, the rejected candidates with reasons, and the sibling decision. Tests: `test/mentions-explain.test.ts`.
- `src/core/verbs/entity-card-identity.ts` — the `entity` card's `aka_sources`, `identity_siblings`, `identity_excerpt` (verbatim lines of the page and its siblings from `sanitizeRemoteBody`, tiered: declarations, label lines with a name-like value, other label lines, lines with another capitalized run or code; 600 characters per page, 1,200 per card, `identity_excerpt_omitted`), `alias_guidance` (surface-aware) and the `identity_siblings_capped` notice. Tests: `test/mentions-identity-siblings.test.ts`.
- `src/core/search/alias-fanout.ts` — search and query's alias fan-out: `resolveQueryEntity` (query n-grams up to 4 tokens through `resolveAliases` and linkable exact titles, longest first, capitalized or code-shaped unless a multi-word n-gram resolves to one page, case-sensitive aliases as written, a tie only when the pages are identity siblings), the names of the entity and its siblings by origin then length, at most `search.alias_fanout_max` (4) keyword queries of 5 rows (`searchAliasRequired`: the name as a required websearch phrase, ranked by the query's other terms through `SearchOpts.rankQuery` on both engines), at most 8 spliced rows with `matched_alias`, and the `fanout` meta (`alias_fanout` notice when capped). Offset 0 only. Tests: `test/search-alias-fanout.test.ts`, `test/search-other-names.test.ts`, `test/e2e/mentions-parity.test.ts`.
- `src/core/schema-migrations/v206-entity-mention-index.ts` — additive: `page_aliases.origin` (`frontmatter` default, `declared`, `subject`), `case_sensitive`, `alias_text`, unique key `(source_id, alias_norm, slug, origin)`; tables `page_mention_state`, `mention_gazetteer_entries`, `mention_index_status` (RLS on when the role bypasses RLS). `GUARDED_TABLE_COLUMNS` lists the new `page_aliases` columns.

Alias readers apply precedence frontmatter > declared > subject per source, and a derived alias never answers for another live page's exact title (`readAliases` in `src/core/search/read-enrichment.ts`, shared by both engines' `resolveAliases`), so `resolveEntitySlug`, the retrieval reflex, the alias hop, intent weights and `search-diagnose` rank an exact title first. `buildGazetteer` (`src/core/by-mention.ts`) applies the same precedence, drops an alias claimed by two pages at its best origin (unless the claimants are identity siblings, which all keep it), yields to a same-source title, drops a single-word alias equal to the first word of another multi-word entity name, applies the generic-word list to every single-word name, and carries `caseTokens` for case-sensitive entries; `opts.dropped` records every dropped name with its reason.
