# Key files: Core Persistence (managed facts)

[Subsystem index](../KEY_FILES.md) · [Core Persistence](core-persistence.md). Read only the entries relevant to your change.
Current behavior and load-bearing invariants; history belongs in Git and CHANGELOG.
Managed facts extraction: the entity-intent preparer and publisher, conversation facts batches and the unmanaged derived index. The extraction pipeline itself is in [Google and Loops (continued)](google-and-loops-continued.md) (`src/core/facts/backstop.ts`) and [Core Services (2/3)](core-services-2.md) (`src/core/facts/extract.ts`).

- `src/core/persistence/{facts-maintenance,facts-prepare}.ts` — the managed facts publisher and preparer behind the backstop's `publishManagedFacts` / `resumeManagedFacts` and the explicit fact writers (`managed-fact-write.ts`, `remember`); the shared maintenance dispatch they ride is in [Core Persistence](core-persistence.md). Each `ManagedFactsSession` carries one `MaintenanceWriteWait` (`maintenance-wait.ts`) built from the caller's `FactsBackstopCtx.deadlineAtMs` (the facts-absorb job passes its deadline; other callers get the 30 s base) and its abort signal: `collectManagedFacts` waits that long for each accepted request through `awaitWrite`, a cancelled job stops waiting at once, and a request still nonterminal after the wait leaves as `write_pending` with its receipt (#6778, `test/facts-absorb-publish-wait-6778.test.ts`). GBRA-130 A-36: `collectManagedFacts` awaits every row before it throws. An entity request that ended on a code of its own page (`ENTITY_REFUSAL_CODES`: `revision_conflict`, `invalid_fence`, `page_body_segmented`, …; not a file-check or withdrawal refusal, which the follow-up walks own) is reported as `refused` (slug, code, request id) on `ManagedFactsResult` and the `extract_facts` result when the session sets `reportRefusals` and another entity of the batch committed (the backstop sets it; `managed-fact-write.ts` and `remember` do not and keep the refusal), and the completion receipt refused only as incomplete is not a failure of its own; any other terminal row, then a pending one, then a refusal with nothing committed beside it, throws as its receipt. Test: `test/pathological/a-36-facts-batch-partial-refusal.test.ts` (PGLite and Postgres). `facts-maintenance.ts` / `facts-prepare.ts` (#6506): an automatic extraction's entity intent carries `withdrawn: 'skip'` (set by the backstop's `publishManagedFacts` call, never by `managed-fact-write.ts` or `remember`), under which `prepareManagedFactsMutation` skips a candidate `isFactWithdrawn` matches (after `assertFactNotPurged`, which still refuses `purged_content`) and counts it as `withdrawn_skipped` on the entity outcome, summed into the completion outcome, `ManagedFactsResult` and the `extract_facts` result (the unmanaged pipeline counts its own B-10 skip into the same field); without the flag the refusal stands. At locked validation the per-entry purge and withdrawal checks run before the page's fence re-validation, and under the flag a claim forgotten since preparation refuses `revision_conflict` with `error.withdrawn` (so the receipt's `error_detail.origin` is `withdrawal`); no `extract_facts` request is re-prepared. `resumeManagedFacts` runs `followWithdrawalRefusals` for an input-keyed session (`inputKeyed`, no request id): the `followFileRefusals` walk generalized as `followRefusals`, matching entity refusals by `withdrawnFromDetail(error_detail)` or the pre-detail `invalid_params` + `FACT_WITHDRAWN_MESSAGE` pair, re-admitting the retained facts under `digest([batchKey, 'withdrawal-retry', n])` with the flag (`readmitWithdrawnFacts`: a fact forgotten at its retained visibility keeps that visibility so preparation skips it instead of narrowing it to private), bounded by `MAX_FILE_REFUSAL_RETRIES`, historical receipts untouched. Tests: `test/managed-facts-withdrawn-skip-6506.test.ts`, `test/managed-facts-withdrawal-recovery-6506.test.ts` (PGLite and Postgres).
- `src/core/facts/same-page-shadow.ts` — #6333 the shadow counter both fact writers share. `samePageRewording(engine, sourceId, fact, embedding)` re-reads the entity's nearest live same-visibility candidate (`listSupersessionCandidates`, the pool and filters of `decideSingleFact`'s cosine pass) and answers whether `wouldDedupSamePage` (capture-dedup.ts: same lane, same `source_session`, same visibility, cosine ≥ 0.85, no diverging guard token) would have called the fact a rewording; `warnSamePageShadow` is the one stderr spelling (`[facts-backstop] #6333 shadow: …; both kept`). `facts-prepare.ts` calls it for every candidate `decideSingleFact` did not match and counts `would_dedup_same_page` on the entity outcome, the completion outcome, `ManagedFactsResult` and the `extract_facts` result; the count is frozen in the receipt, so a replay reports it and does not recount. Nothing is dropped; this is the managed mirror of the unmanaged backstop's counter (`backstop.ts`), which never fired on a managed brain. Tests: `test/backstop-same-page-shadow-managed-6333.test.ts` (PGLite and Postgres), `test/backstop-same-page-shadow-6333.test.ts` (unmanaged).
- `src/core/persistence/derived-facts.ts` — `writeDerivedFacts`: the unmanaged conversation fact index commits in one maintenance transaction under the maintenance principal; on a managed brain it refuses with `writer_coordinator_required`, because every managed facts/takes writer publishes a receipted maintenance request (`test/receipt-coverage.test.ts` lists every coordinated write site). Tests: `test/write-attribution-misc.test.ts`.
- `src/core/facts/conversation-publication.ts` — managed conversation facts as database-only `managed_maintenance_conversation_facts` batch requests (`submitDatabaseMaintenanceIntent` on `ANCHOR_SLUG`, a dot-led slug no page can hold, dispatched from `prepareMaintenanceMutation`). Each page's frozen entry (`buildConversationPage`: `FrozenExtractedFact` rows, its generation and attempt, vectors only under the brain's facts embedding signature) joins the run's batch; `enqueueManagedPage` publishes it once `DEFAULT_BATCH_CAPS` is reached (25 pages, 10 with extracted facts, 8 MiB) and `flushManagedBatch` at the end of the run. The generation key (protocol, source incarnation, page id, revision, parser-input version token, `CONVERSATION_EXTRACTOR_VERSION`, selectors, newest extractor row id) is checked before model work by `startManagedPage` against `loadGenerationIndex` (the writer's unsettled and failed batches on the anchor, read once per run): a member of an unsettled batch waits and replays its receipt, a member of a retryably failed batch is resubmitted from its stored entry, a deterministic failure of a batch that held it alone or three failed batches block it (GBRA-130 A-51: `publishBatch` re-publishes a multi-page batch that failed deterministically in halves, so only the page that fails alone takes the code; `test/pathological/a-51-conversation-batch-booking.test.ts`); all without model calls. The batch request id digests its members' (generation, attempt) pairs. The preparer locks every member's key and applies each on its own (`applyEntry`): a deleted, replaced or moved page is skipped and reported, a page whose rows fail validation gets a durable `blocked:` not-extractable outcome, vectors under another signature are dropped; none fails the batch. `estimateConversationIntentBytes`/`conversationIntentByteLimit` skip a page whose largest entry cannot fit before any model call; `assertConversationAdmissionHeadroom` stops admitting at 80% of the writer's outstanding requests, reserved receipt bytes or lifetime ids (`maintenance_backpressure`, exit 12, naming the resource and a `--limit` that fits), up front for the run's planned batches and before each new batch. `publishConversationFacts` (the managed apply and the unmanaged writer) passes fact rows through the write gate at the page's derived tier and retires the prior batch only for a complete replacement (#7013: `full` scope, at least one fact, nothing held or rejected) or on a page with no active extractor facts, through `replaceConversationFacts`, which deletes the page's extractor rows (expiring referenced and keeping label-retired ones) and inserts the rows numbered above every remaining row, failing on a lost insert. Otherwise the prior facts stay: the outcome row replaces the page's prior outcome rows (so the page is not paid for again), a `window` run (`--since` dropped messages) adds its facts beside them, and a `partial` run (a `--segment-limit` cut, a page changed mid-run) publishes no facts over them. Each entry carries its `scope`; an entry frozen without one is `partial` without its outcome, `window` with a `--since` selector, else `full`. Tests: `test/pathological/h4-conversation-facts-zero-yield.test.ts` (managed and unmanaged, + Postgres arm). Tests: `test/conversation-facts-managed.test.ts` (+ Postgres arm), managed-connector contract cases `extract_conversation_facts*`.
- `src/core/facts/subject-infer.ts` — zero-LLM fact subject inference shared by the write paths (remember, the facts backstop) and `gbrain facts relink`. `inferFactSubject(engine, sourceId, { fact, pageSlug?, mode, excludePrivate? })` returns `{ slug, via: 'page' | 'mention' }` or a miss reason (`ambiguous`, `unverified_match`, `no_mention`, `no_page`). Page tier: the trusted page the fact came from, when it is a live fact-entity page. Mention tier: strong `extractCandidates` names plus slugs written in the text (lowercase Unicode page-slug letters without combining marks; a literal glued to further letters is not a literal), resolved through `resolveStrictEntityReference` (exact slug, unique basename, unique alias; `relink` mode adds the same-name trigram arm); exactly one entity links. Any other real name that does not resolve to the same entity (including names with no page and bare first names) vetoes the link; sentence-start capitals, weekdays/months and role terms (CEO, Q3) do not. `excludePrivate` removes private pages before uniqueness is counted. Also `appendContextNote`/`contextHead` (the `" — "` note convention), `recordedPageSlug` (page provenance of page-extraction rows only) and `isEntityInferenceEnabled` (`facts.entity_inference` kill switch for write-time inference; relink ignores it). `resolveStrictEntityReference` in `entities/resolve.ts` never guesses from a bare-name prefix and never slug-falls-back. Pinned by `test/facts-subject-infer.test.ts` (a 40+ fact labeled fixture: write precision >= 0.99, relink free tiers >= 0.95).
- `src/core/facts/conversation-subject-infer.ts` — #6764 write-time subject inference for `gbrain extract-conversation-facts` (both the classic and managed branches, trusted local only) and the free conversation relink. `inferConversationSubject` is the per-fact rule (a withdrawn claim misses as `withdrawn`); `inferConversationSubjects(engine, sourceId, visibility, rows)` gives each row the extractor left without an entity the slug `inferFactSubject` proves in `write` mode (mention tier; `excludePrivate` for world rows) plus the `entity inferred from <tier>` context note, unless `facts.entity_inference` is off or the claim was withdrawn. The row stays a derived row of the conversation page (`entity_slug` column only). Test: `test/conversation-facts-unlinked-6764.test.ts`.
- `src/core/facts/conversation-relink.ts` — `gbrain facts relink --conversation [--source] [--slugs] [--dry-run] [--json]` (#6764, dispatched from `src/commands/facts.ts`): the free relink of the conversation extractor's unlinked derived rows, which plain relink never examines. Pages are the named `--slugs`, else the 50 with the most unlinked rows not yet tried (`conversationRelinkPages`). Each row runs `inferConversationSubject` (write mode, visibility-filtered, withdrawn claims skipped; no model). A page's links are one write: a managed brain publishes one database-only `managed_maintenance_conversation_relink` request on the anchor `.maintenance/conversation-relink` holding the page key (`prepareConversationRelink`, dispatched in `persistence/prepared-maintenance.ts`; the request id comes from the planned links, and a failed attempt retries under the next id), an unmanaged brain one `writeDerivedFacts` transaction. The write sets `entity_slug` and appends `entity relinked from <tier>` only on rows still exactly as planned (same claim, unlinked, active, target page live) and records a `linked` attempt; unresolved rows get a `conversation_unresolved` memo in `fact_relink_attempts` (reason the miss), which moves doctor `unlinked_facts` to the paid re-extraction fallback, and the report prints that command for their pages. Test: `test/conversation-relink-6764.test.ts`.
- `src/core/facts/conversation-memo.ts` — #6764 what makes free relink, then paid re-extraction, then linking by hand converge, all in `fact_relink_attempts`: `conversationTriedSql` (a `conversation_unresolved` memo, re-armed for the free relink by any page created in the source after it unless `RELINK_REASONS` memoizes the reason), `conversationPaidSql` and `recordReextractMemo` (tier `paid_reextract`, `target_slug` the page, `reason` its `content_hash`, keyed on the page's completion outcome row; written when an extraction of the page completes, unmanaged in `extract-conversation-facts.ts` and managed in `conversation-publication.ts` `applyEntry`). No schema change. Test: `test/conversation-memo-6764.test.ts`.
- `src/core/facts/conversation-relink-carry.ts` — #6764 how a free conversation relink survives a batch replacement: `clearConversationFacts` (conversation-publication.ts) expires the page's relinked rows (`RELINKED_DERIVED_ROW`: entity set and `entity relinked from` in the context) instead of deleting them, after deleting the previous clear's expired copies, and `inheritRelinkedEntities` gives each inserted row with no entity whose claim matches one of them that entity (live page only) and the relink note, in every insert `publishConversationFacts` makes (managed and unmanaged, replacement or addition) and in `insertGatedFacts`. One active copy, link kept. Test: `test/conversation-relink-6764.test.ts`.
