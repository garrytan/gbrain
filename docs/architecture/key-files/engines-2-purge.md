# Key files: Engines (2/2, purge)

[Subsystem index](../KEY_FILES.md) · [Previous entries](engines-2-last.md). Read only the entries relevant to your change.
Current behavior and load-bearing invariants; history belongs in Git and CHANGELOG.

- `src/core/engine-sql/head-blocker.ts` — the head-blocker probe of `persistence/head-blocker.ts` as typed engine members: `readHeadBlockerRows(ids, hostId, { priority })` (one row per queued request asked about: whether it is claimable now, its transient backoff, the worktree owner and state, refresh fence, effect and request recoveries, pending withdrawal mirrors, and the earlier and ahead requests that hold it) and `readRecoveringHeadRow(id)` (the recovery record and worktree owner of a recovering head). The text splices `persistence-claims.ts` `claimableWriteFragment` and `foregroundPriorityFragment`; `test/persistence-journal-statements.test.ts` pins it against the journal fragments. The Rust store answers `readHeadBlockerRows` from its records (rust/docs/store/persistence.md "Head-blocker probe") and `readRecoveringHeadRow` is null there, since a Rust brain has no worktree. Pinned by `test/engine-parity/persistence.test.ts`.
- `src/core/engine-sql/label-door.ts` — the reads and the one write delete_page's derived-origin carry and the label door make, as typed engine members: `readPrivacyOrigin` (a page that is an atom or that the privacy rules hide, live or soft-deleted), `listOriginDependents` (private-labels.ts `dependentsSql('remove')`: the pages a removal of the origins would open), `listCheckoutFiles` (each listed page with a recorded file and its source's checkout, by id), `readPageLabelRow` (a page's id and stored frontmatter) and `writePageLabelGuard` (sets or deletes `page_label_guards`). `repair/origin-carry.ts`, `repair/label-write.ts` and `trust/page-write.ts` call them and surface an `engine_capability_missing` refusal. The Rust store serves all five (rust/docs/store/label_door.md); the explicit purge still refuses there before any write. Pinned by `test/engine-parity/delete-page.test.ts`. `readLabelGuardTiers` is the label guard's tier read (`trust/page-write.ts` `labelGuardTier`, a typed preparation read): the owner-set guard and the open `lower_page` prior tiers.
- `src/core/engine-sql/page-purge.ts` — the reads and tombstone edits of a page purge (`persistence/page-purge.ts`) and of `gbrain pages purges list` and `gbrain pages unpurge`, as typed engine members: the page's claims (`listPurgeClaimFacts`, `listPurgeClaimTakes`, `listPurgeSegmentClaims`: the facts filed on it or extracted from it, outcome markers apart, and its takes), `listQueuedPagePurges` (the queued purges behind one, each with its page id), `listPageLinkStreams`, the stored body and versions (`readPurgePageBody`, `listPageVersionBodiesForPurge`, `listPageVersionSourcePaths`, `countPageCascadeRows`), the mirrored claims (`listPageMirrorFacts`, `listPageMirrorTakeHashes`) and the tombstones (`insertPageFactPurges`, `listPagePurgeTombstones`, `listPagePurgeRows`, `deletePagePurgeTombstones`, `deleteFactPurgesByRequests`, `deleteTakePurgesByRequests`). `PAGE_FACTS` (the page's facts as one predicate) and `PAGE_PURGE_TOMBSTONES_SQL` live here; statements with no order sort in the function so every engine lists alike. A Rust brain serves them from `rust-engine/store-members/page-purge.ts` over gbrain-store `facts/page_purge_reads.rs` and the ledgers (`rust/docs/store/facts.md`). Pinned by `test/engine-parity/page-purge-reads.test.ts` and `rust/crates/gbrain-store/tests/page_purge_reads.rs`.
- `src/core/engine-sql/page-purge-sweep.ts` — the deletes and reads of a page purge's sweep and of its segment storage, as typed engine members the Rust engine serves from its store (`rust/docs/store/facts.md`, "The page purge sweep, its segment storage and its slug stores"): `deletePurgedPageTrustProposals`, `deletePurgedPageHolds`, `deletePurgedPageGateReceipts`, `deletePurgedPageFacts`, `deletePurgedPageFiles` (their `RETURNING` rows sorted), `listPageTimelineReceipts` (the add_timeline_entry and edit_page receipts), `listPageSegmentShas`, `countPageManifestRows`, `deletePageStagings`, `deletePageSegmentTexts` and `sweepPageTextsForClaims` (one statement of the #9743 claim sweep). The review queue, review proposal and decide proposal deletes of the sweep are the fact purge's members (`facts-purge.ts`). `persistence/purge-slug-stores.ts` reports both secret store counts as 0 on a brain with no secret store.
- `src/core/engine-sql/page-purge-journal.ts` — the journal statements of a page purge's sweep and intent redaction (`persistence/intent-redaction.ts`), as typed engine members the Rust engine serves from its request, effect and counter records (`rust/docs/store/persistence.md`, "The page purge's journal statements"): `listRequestsForSlugs`, `readJournalCeiling`, `sweepJournalChunk` (one keyset chunk of the carrying predicate), `lockWriteRequestsById` (main's member, shared), `readEffectBytes`, `redactWriteRequestRow` and `releaseTerminalBytes`. The two sweep reads run in write preparation, so they are in `TYPED_PREPARATION_READS` and end on the server at the claim's deadline.
- `src/core/engine-sql/purge-stores.ts` — the scrubs of a page purge's retrieval stores, name stores and query cache, as typed engine members (`rust/docs/store/eval_capture.md`, "The page purge's retrieval, name and cache scrubs"): `deletePurgedFeedbackRows` (the retrieval event, link event, rating and weight deletes and the `eval_candidates.retrieved_slugs` rewrite of `scrubPurgedSlugs`, five counts keyed as the purge receipt names them), `deletePurgedNameStoreRows` (the volunteer log and gazetteer of `scrubPurgedNameStores`) and `clearQueryCache` (null when the table is missing; `clearRebuildableCache` sends only `query_cache` here). The Rust engine runs the eval candidate scrub in its store and answers 0 for the stores it does not keep. `purgeStoreScrubber(tx)` adapts a raw transaction for the engines' own purge SQL (`engine-sql/pages.ts`). `src/core/engine-sql/purge-receipt.ts` holds the two reads of the receipt and the completion as typed members: `relationExists` (`to_regclass`; `rebuildable-cache.ts` and `purge-verify.ts` call it through the helper of the same name) and `readDerivationPurgeState` (the stalled or pending record of a request's derivation sweep; `derivationPurgeState` in `segment-derive.ts` calls it). The Rust engine answers both in TypeScript (`rust/docs/store/pages.md`, "The page purge's receipt reads"): true for a relation the graduation inventory lists, null for the sweep state.
- `src/core/engine-sql/origin-holds.ts` — the statements of a derived-origin purge's parks and owed-purge records, as typed engine members the Rust engine serves from its page records and op checkpoint rows (`rust/docs/store/label_door.md`, "The purge's origin parks and owed-purge records"): `listPurgeOrigins` (the in-purge recheck's read of the origins among the ids, bounded like the carry's other origin reads), `upsertDerivedOriginPark` (a re-park keeps the owner request an earlier park held), `recordOwedPurgeRows` (the owed record and the hold's tombstone binding, rows written per record) and `deleteOriginHoldRows` (the park or owed rows of pages; only those two ops). `persistence/derived-origin-hold.ts` `parkDerivedOrigin`, `recordOwedPurge`, `clearOwedPurges` and `clearDerivedOriginParks` take the members instead of a raw engine. They run through `executeRaw` on the handle given, so inside a purge transaction they join it. The prunes and the owed-purge read of the same rows go through `engine-sql/retention-purge.ts`. Pinned by `test/engine-parity/page-purge-holds.test.ts` and `rust/crates/gbrain-store/tests/page_purge_holds.rs`.
- `src/core/engine-sql/retention-purge.ts` — the retention purge's bookkeeping, as typed engine members the Rust engine serves from its page records and op checkpoint rows (`rust/docs/store/label_door.md`, "The retention purge's bookkeeping"); the texts live in `persistence/retention-purge-sql.ts`: `releaseStaleOriginParkHolds`, `listOwedPurgeRecords`, `pruneOwedPurgeRecords` and `pruneOriginParkRecords` (`persistence/derived-origin-hold.ts`), `prunePurgeParkRecords`, `recordPurgeFailureRecord`, `deletePurgeParkRecord`, `markPurgeParksInactive` and `dropRearmedPurgeParks` (`persistence/purge-parks.ts`), `listReleasedPurgeOrigins` and `listManagedPurgeCandidates` (`persistence/purge-deleted.ts` `selectPurgeCandidates`) and `listExpiringOriginPages` (`repair/origin-carry.ts`, bounded like the carry's other origin reads). Each runs on the handle given, so inside a purge transaction it joins it. Pinned by `test/engine-sql-caller-raw-sql-pins.test.ts`, `test/engine-parity/retention-purge.test.ts` and `rust/crates/gbrain-store/tests/retention_purge.rs`.
