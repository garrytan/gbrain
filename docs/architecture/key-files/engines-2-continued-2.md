- `src/shadow/` + `src/commands/engine-shadow.ts` — Rust engine shadow mode: sampled reads compared in the background, plus a seeded Rust store that follows the brain's writes, and the writer evidence the swap check needs (ENGINES.md "Rust engine shadow mode"). `shadow/status-document.ts` `shadowStatusDocument` builds the one status object `gbrain engine shadow status --json` prints and the engine swap reads (`readConfiguredShadowStatus`: null when the brain has no `state.json`, which the swap reports as `no_shadow`). `shadow/state.ts` (`shadowStateFor(config)`) reads `configDir()/shadow/<brain>/state.json` (brain key `<engine>-<12 hex>` from `brainIdentity`) and returns null when shadow mode is off, the file is missing or unreadable, or `GBRAIN_SHADOW_CHILD=1`; `engine-factory.ts` calls it and, only when it is on, dynamically imports `shadow/attach.ts`, which wraps the engine (`shadow/wrapper.ts`) so a sampled read or search (`shadow/reads.ts`) is answered by the primary first and then offered to a bounded fail-open `MirrorQueue` (`shadow/mirror.ts`: sheds on overflow, never awaits) as a capture function the queue runs synchronously when it has room, before the caller sees the result; the arguments are encoded (`shadow/codec.ts`, functions and signals dropped) before the primary call and the result inside the capture, so later changes by the caller cannot make a false diff; a sampled read encodes its arguments even if the queue sheds it, and only an accepted read also encodes its result; calls inside a transaction are skipped. `shadow/supervisor.ts` (`ShadowSupervisor`) spawns `gbrain engine shadow __child <dir>` lazily through `src/core/spawn.ts`, unref'd, with stderr to `child.log`, restarts it with doubling backoff, disables mirroring after 5 exits in 10 minutes (`restart_budget_spent`), kills a child silent for 60 s, refuses lines past an 8 MiB stdin buffer (write samples are held to 8 MiB still in the pipe, watched through their write callbacks because Bun's `writableLength` reads 0), write sample lines are staged and one `setImmediate` flush per event-loop turn writes `write-owed.<session>.json` (everything accepted for the child, and `owed_qualifying`, the page and link writes among it by `sampleQualifyingWeight`, flushed file and directory, never inside the caller's request) before it hands the staged lines to the pipe in their original order; plain weight-1 samples share one record: the first writes a `v:2` open marker (`open_until`, through the `writeOwed` seam) and the record stating the whole count (`v:1`) is written at the close (`OWED_WINDOW_MS` 100 ms, `OWED_WINDOW_SAMPLES` 256, `drain`, `stop`, exit, or any known-loss line or `owe`; option `owedWindowMs` 0 keeps the per-turn flush), about two durable writes (four fsyncs) per save, where the per-turn flush paid one per turn; a marker whose write failed refuses its sample and may stand, so `markerStray` makes the next flush (or a `retryMs` timer, `armStrayRepair`) replace it with a record of what was accepted, even with nothing staged, and a failed close keeps the lines staged and retries; `closing` freezing admission while a close is in doubt (a weighted line is refused as `send_shed`, `owe` raises the count and publishes at once), and a `v:2` record at an orphan pass books `missing - 1` as `receipt_orphaned` and one `write_window_lost` (`write_windows_lost` in status, criterion 5 no evidence; `WriteTally.missing(n, windowLost)`, never bucketed into `other`; the booked window is kept as the WINDOW BLOCKER, a file of its own (`write-owed.window-lost-<session>.json`, `WINDOW_BLOCKER_PREFIX`: the shape of a tombstone, `v: 2` with `booked_epoch`, plus `window_target`, the open marker's `owed`, `booked_through` / `booked_qualifying`, a copy of the credit banked in the tally's `settled_before` / `settled_before_qualifying`, and the session's `serving_pid`): only the owner pass (`WriteTally.obligations`), the owner worker's own booking (`WriteTally.missing`) and the tally fold write it, the owner pass removes a booked one once its epoch is over (`removeStaleTomb`), and `removeSettledOwed`, called by the owner pass and by the owner worker's end of input (`finish()`), drops one whose window was never booked over a marker that closed normally; every removal holds the tally lock. `holdBlocker` writes it BEFORE the window is credited (so no settled marker lacks an epoch; a window another epoch's blocker already booked is never booked again, and an unbooked one is taken over for the new epoch), the tally fold writes it as an intent only (no credit is published before the tally that holds it is renamed), a session's settled count is the sum of three disjoint parts (`settledOf`: the credit banked before its entry restarted, `anchorOf`; the tally's `settled` entry; the lines nobody has folded), never a comparison of sizes, whatever epoch the blocker names, an unreadable blocker lookup in the fold aborts it (nothing committed), as does an unreadable session record there (`servingPidOf` throws rather than stamp the folding worker's own pid on the blocker), and an orphan pass that finds a client journal unreadable settles nothing that round (`unfoldedLines` returns `unreadable`), and judges which sessions are gone (child exited, or record over 10 minutes old, closed or without its serving process) before the journal read (one gone later waits a pass; a live stale child writing after the read counts twice), the retired credit of a batch never includes its window (`Batch.windowCredit`, so the next epoch's owner books the window once), `readWriteSnapshot` repeats its read when a blocker appeared after its listing, and the stale-blocker cleanup waits for a pending marker named for the session, the window is booked LAST of the first `window_target` samples (`bookOrphan`'s `head`) so the settled count reaches the target exactly when the window is booked, `removeSettledOwed` (the locked remover) banks the settled count (`bankSettled`: one tally rename that also restarts the entry), copies it to the blocker and then deletes the settled session file (a blocker whose window was never booked, over a marker that closed normally, is dropped), and `readWriteSnapshot` counts a booked blocker once per session as `windowFiles` (status's `write_windows_lost` is the larger of the tally's `write_window_lost` count and `windowFiles`). A blocker is kept until its epoch is over, when a pass of a later epoch removes it (`removeStaleTomb`, under the same tally lock, on the record read again there: it deletes only a blocker or tombstone of an earlier epoch, and only while the manifest read again there still names the epoch the pass began in (a pass the epoch has moved past leaves the file; it may be the new epoch's blocker), and a republished, higher record stays, and a blocker whose session's serving process still runs stays too; `removeOwed` is the bare deletion both removers call, always under the lock). The supervisor never writes that name, so no supervisor write path (`putOwed`'s lock-free one included) can drop it. Tombstones in the session's own file (`booked_epoch` on `write-owed.<session>.json`, builds up to 424b0fa0cf) are read in place and kept as before; nothing converts them. an older build's over-count only keeps the write check closed and does not reset or extend shadow days (the lost-window count is not an epoch input); a failed close is a failed owed write, so `keep` and the write-pending marker below apply to it; a failed write keeps the lines staged and retries, and a stage bound counts every kind. At disconnect `drain` waits up to 2 s for the pipe to flush, and `closeOwed` closes the record (`closed`; `child_exited` is written when the child dies) so the child books any sample it never read as `pipe_unflushed` (capability_missing `pipe_unflushed_nonqualifying` for any other write; an open marker's one extra sample counts as a page and link write, and is the one booked as `write_window_lost`). `shadow/write-compare.ts` keeps the other half: `WriteTally.note` takes the credit a verdict settles and saves it with the verdict in one locked rename (the child defers that save one event-loop turn (`begin`, `missing` and the end-of-input save stay immediate), so an outcome unsaved at its death is booked `receipt_orphaned` and a replay's mark stays until its ending save) (`settled[session]` and its page-and-link part `settled_qualifying[session]`, carried to a new epoch with `lost_applied` and `applied`), a client or retired child writes `no_epoch`/`epoch_retired` lost lines that name the session (`q: 1` marks a page or link write; numbered per writer, so a retried batch repeats its numbers and the owner takes each once, in `line_seq`), `readWriteSnapshot` reads tally, then unfolded lost lines, then owed files, then the tally's identity again so status can only over-block, and `WriteTally.obligations` (at start, each save and every 10 s) books a gone session's `owed - settled` once as `receipt_orphaned`, split by `owed_qualifying` less `settled_qualifying` (the rest is capability_missing `receipt_orphaned_nonqualifying`; `LOST_NONQUALIFYING` folds those lines), and removes its file only after a tally read back from disk covers it; the child removes its own closed file at the end of its input under the same rule, and an open `v: 2` marker it settled the same way, once its booking has held the window's blocker for its own epoch. Legacy `write-sent.*` receipts, lost lines with no session (`legacy_lines`) and `sent_count n=`/`no_epoch n=` faults from older writers are still read as no evidence. The supervisor stops when `state.json` turns off. `shadow/child.ts` (`runShadowChild`) loads the addon offline (`shadow/addon.ts` `loadRustEngineAddonOffline`, the cache-only loader: `not_installed` when no addon is cached or overridden, no network), opens the Rust engine, replays each read, classifies it (`shadow/classify.ts`: `match`, `order_diff`, `diff`, `stale`, `inconclusive`, `collation_order`, `privacy_diverged`, `capability_missing`, `error`, `timeout`, `addon_unavailable`) and appends content-free rows to the local ledger (`shadow/ledger.ts`); a privacy divergence pauses comparison and tells the parent to stop mirroring. `shadow/report.ts` (`buildProvisionalReport`) totals the per-child summary files (`summaries/`), not the ledger rows, into a report whose verdict is always `NOT READY`; `gbrain engine shadow report` writes `reports/provisional.{json,md}` only after the shadow directory exists. `commands/engine-shadow.ts` (`runEngineShadow`) is `gbrain engine shadow start|status|stop|report|seed|reconcile|drill|bench`; `start` previews a plan and its `plan_hash`, needs `--yes --expect <hash>` (a missing or stale hash refuses `preview_changed`), and refuses without writing when the real addon loader refuses. `start` also reads the brain's storage mode once, best-effort (`shadow/managed-gate.ts` `readManagedStorage`; a brain that cannot be opened or read is `managed: null` with a reason code), prints a line for a managed brain, which cannot swap, and records `storage: { managed, checked_at, reason? }` in `state.json`; `status --json` and the report carry it as `storage` with a `note`. Only the swap refuses over storage. `commands/doctor/checks/rust-engine-addon.ts` (`rust_engine_addon`) reports the offline addon load only when shadow mode is on. Its only brain-database writes are the swap-eligibility writer evidence; nothing is written to brain content, the config table or the brain repo. `shadow/writer-observations.ts` notes one `engine_writer_observations` row per writing process (host id, connection name, command kind, version, `write_protocol` (`WRITE_PROTOCOL`, 2 in this build; migration 340 adds the nullable column with no default and no backfill, so an older build's row stays NULL), first and last write), after the write committed and at most every 10 minutes, only while `state.json` says shadow mode is on. `shadow/writer-hooks.ts` is Postgres-only: `start --yes` installs the statement-level `gbrain_writer_note` trigger on every brain table in one transaction (`writer-hooks.json` is written first) and `stop` removes it; the trigger records each writing connection in `engine_writer_sessions` and every failure or skipped refresh in `engine_writer_coverage_gaps`, never fails a write, waits at most 50 ms on its own tables. `shadow/eligibility.ts` (`buildEligibilityStatus`) fills the status document's `eligibility` fields (`writer_hosts_7d`, `unknown_writer_paths` and `legacy_write_writers` only when the caller passes the open brain, `team_brain`, `preview_target`; a failed read leaves the writer fields out and adds `eligibility_evidence` with the error code), and `shadow/writer-coverage.ts` adds the file-side evidence (reconcile misses, repairs under an invalid watermark, a lost note, torn records). A writer row seen since the evidence epoch began (its start less the 10 minute refresh slack; the 7-day window does not cut it off) with no `write_protocol`, or one below `WRITE_PROTOCOL`, is one more unknown path and is named in `legacy_write_writers`; so is an epoch manifest that is there and cannot be read (`readEpochState` tells `absent`, which means no epoch yet and keeps the window start, from `unreadable`), and a brain where migration 340 has not run cannot read the evidence at all, which leaves the writer fields out. `gbrain engine shadow status` never opens the database. Swap-readiness evidence: `shadow/evidence-epoch.ts` keeps `evidence/epoch.json` (seed id, addon SHA-256, comparator and adapter versions, capability-set hash, readiness-policy hash; a changed input opens a new epoch, an unrelated release does not) and the child's content-free `rust_internal_error` fault recorder; ledger rows, usage lines and faults carry the epoch id. `shadow/exactness.ts` is the one table of comparisons that must match exactly (every `exact` read, `findByTitleFuzzy`, and the `ts_rank` keyword and title searches; `searchTakes` and `searchTakesVector` count only a different row set, and `searchVector` only a diff under `excludePrivate` whose extra Rust rows did not all pass the per-row privacy check); a mismatch there is `stale` only when the child's follower held older committed, unapplied work, else `inconclusive` and gets a stable rerun. `shadow/usage.ts` and the `UsageMeter` in `mirror.ts` count every call per operation class (offered, sampled out, shed, in a transaction, unmirrorable) into `usage/<day>.jsonl`. `shadow/evidence.ts` `buildEvidenceStatus` (spread into `shadowStatusDocument`) folds the current epoch's ledger, usage files and whole fault history into `reads`, `privacy_diverged`, `search.trigram_exact_disagreements`, `search.keyword_title`, `search.vector_recall_at_10`, `search.primary_recall_at_10` and `child` (absent, never zero, when evidence is missing: `privacy_diverged` also while a privacy check of the last 7 days is unsettled, the recall fields until 30 samples are scored) and prints the epoch start, the earliest READY date and the 100-per-class engineering default. Epoch opening is serialized across processes by `evidence/epoch.lock`; the epoch names the addon the child loaded, so a child that is only a client of another process's owner (whose addon digest the owner handshake does not carry) opens none and its rows count for nothing, until it takes over. `SHADOW_ADAPTER_VERSION` (pinned to a digest of the adapter source by a test) is an epoch input. Serving processes append each operation class's first use per epoch at once (`usage/`), and the follower's probe, fingerprint totals, allocator result, lag histograms and reconcile passes are tagged with the evidence epoch and reported only for the current one. An unparsable or malformed fault line makes the child fields absent. `follower.json` names its seed in `seed_id`; the status `epoch_id` and `epoch_started_at` come from the manifest. The manifest also records `build` (stamped gbrain commit, version, addon target) once when the epoch opens; it is never an epoch input, and an epoch from before the field has none (unknown). A text-ordered read (`getTags`, `listPages` with `sort: 'slug'`) whose rows are the primary's re-sorted in C byte order is `collation_order`: explained and counted as successful coverage, with its own count per class in `evidence.collation_order`; never an unexplained divergence. Criterion 10's producers: `shadow/drill.ts` `runBackupDrill` (`gbrain engine shadow drill [--backup]`) refuses `addon_mismatch` unless the addon it loads has the SHA-256 the evidence epoch recorded (stored in the record as `addon_sha256`; hashed through `loadedAddonPath`, the child's own resolver, so an addon installed as `<engine_build>/<target>-<sha256>.node` hashes the same in both), takes `drills/drill.lock` (written whole; a dead holder's lock is taken only through `drills/drill.lock.takeover`, an unreadable or live one never; it is held until the result is written and the pending marker removed, on every path, and removed only while it is still this drill's own; `takeDrillLockForDelete` takes the same lock for `stop --delete`, which `commands/engine-shadow.ts` `stopWithDrillLock` holds until the shadow is deleted, so a delete never runs under a drill and a first drill never starts under it), asks the shadow's owner (`snapshotStore`, a client of the child's owner socket or in process when the store is free) for one copy at one generation under `drills/scratch-<id>/` (it never pauses the shadow), deletes the scratch copy only once the owner has ended it (after a copy call that failed it waits up to 30 s for `snapshotStatus` to leave `running`, each status request bounded by the time left, so an owner that stops answering counts as still running), checks the copy with `verifyStoreDir` (no normal open), opens it once (phase 2: `probeCopy` lists pages with the search-excluded slug prefixes left out before the page limit, reads each page's chunks in the page's own source, drops the engine's stop words (`shadow/english-stopwords.ts`, a copy of `stem.rs` that a test compares word for word) before it counts its tries, tries the longest letter-only words until the engine answers one, and records `no_probe_term`, never a pass, when a populated copy whose keyword index is complete got no hit (an incomplete index may miss, but a query must have run); a page count; its generation after the open kept apart as `post_open`) and, only while the shadow directory it started in still exists (else `shadow_gone`, nothing written), writes `drills/backup.json` (`method: online_logical`, epoch id, sizes, digests compared, `scratch_deleted` read back from disk, `pass`, `reason`) and `history.jsonl`; `drills/pending.json` (op id, drill id, scratch path) is written before the copy is asked for and removed only once the scratch copy is gone and this drill's result is durable (or the shadow is gone), so status reads `cleanup_pending` for as long as it exists; a result that cannot be written is `drill_error` and also removes an earlier `backup.json`, and a copy the owner has not ended leaves its scratch, a record with `scratch_deleted: false` and the marker. A later drill clears an old marker only once the owner reports that copy ended or is gone (else `cleanup_pending`, nothing written): it removes the old scratch directory, then the old `backup.json` durably, then the marker, and when either removal cannot be done it is `cleanup_failed` with the marker kept and nothing written. `shadow/eval-gates.ts` is the eval half: `fetchEvalGates` (`drill --evals`, the only network use; it needs the shadow directory and epoch to exist, never creates the shadow root and writes nothing, reason `shadow_gone`, once that directory or `drills/` was deleted or replaced) downloads the release's `gbrain-eval-gates.pin.json` and the file it names (commit-pinned raw URL, SHA-256, size and redirect limits) into `drills/`; `evalGatesReason` decides again on every read (FINAL only, E1 to E3 PASS, the epoch's starting commit and addon SHA-256, the store's format, this host's target). `shadow/drill-status.ts` `buildDrillStatus` (spread into `shadowStatusDocument`) turns both into `drills.backup_restore_passed` and `drills.eval_gates_e1_e3` with reasons, reading files only. `scripts/eval-gates-pin.ts` and `.github/workflows/eval-gates-pin.yml` (every 3 hours; it reads the newest 100 releases) attach the pin to the release. Privacy, rerun and recall: `shadow/checks.ts` answers the child's `ask` lines in the serving process (per-item read-policy check under the captured `CallerAccess`, stable-generation rerun behind a follow-stream barrier, exact vector reference), `shadow/child-checks.ts` is the child's side (pending answers, privacy verdict, bounded rerun queue), `shadow/samples.ts` is the bounded sample registry the child asks about by opaque id, `shadow/recall.ts` scores recall@10 against the reference (30 samples per epoch). `shadow/bench.ts` (`classifyBench`, `buildBenchStatus`, spread into `shadowStatusDocument`) turns the stored engine-bench speed summary into readiness criterion 8's `bench` fields (gbrain-evals `criterion8()` rule over every expected operation class and metric, against both TypeScript engines; anything not measured is named and `rust_no_worse` stays false; `filtered_vector_margin_met` is never written), `shadow/bench-receipt.ts` validates the receipt and `importBenchReceipt` stores the reduced summary (`commands/engine-shadow.ts` `bench --import` calls it, and only when the brain already has a shadow), and `shadow/bench-file.ts` reads a regular file only, bounded, without blocking. An owed record that could not be made durable (`shadow/supervisor.ts` `keep`, `tickKept`, `oweRefused`) is kept in the serving process, marked by `write-pending.<session>.json` (`shadow/write-compare.ts` `pendingPath`), and published and cleared under the tally lock (a kept record carries `owed_qualifying` like a written one, and the keeper's written-mark includes it; the `oweRefused` record states none, so every sample it holds counts as a page and link write, as `owe` counts one of unknown kind); status blocks on a marker, and every owed-file removal (`removeSettledOwed`) re-reads the record and the marker under that lock. The wrapper sees the `BrainEngine` method calls on a transaction callback's engine, and the statements sent on its driver handle (`tx.sql` as a tagged template or `unsafe`, `tx.db.query` / `exec`, on PGLite the `tx.db.sql` template), which `shadow/wrapper.ts` `viewOfDriver` sends through the same wrapping as `executeRaw` as the text the driver sends (a postgres.js query through its own `handle` when it is first awaited or started, so a fragment is never sent alone and `execute()`, `forEach()` and cursors wait behind a pre-read like an await; a structural value it cannot render makes the write an invalidation; `describe()` is a read). The handle's other members, including ones that send a statement (postgres.js `begin`, whose callback runs on an unwrapped child handle, `savepoint`, `file`, `notify`; PGLite `transaction`, `listen`), and a driver a helper reaches some other way are invisible to it: `test/shadow-driver-accessor-guard.test.ts` (scan in `test/helpers/driver-accessor-scan.ts`) walks every `src/` function reachable from a `transaction` / `transactionDirect` callback (`SHADOW_TRANSACTION_MEMBERS`, helpers and transaction wrappers followed) and fails on any driver accessor that is not a reviewed row in its `REVIEWED` table (two today, each with its reason; a row that no longer matches fails too). Short serve sessions: a serving process that closes with everything handed over seals the follower (`shadow/seal.ts`, `follow-child.ts` "Sealing"), and the next owner session carries the watermark on only after the seal's file checks pass and, on Postgres, its serving process confirms that the primary shows no write since the seal (`seal.ts`). On Postgres the seal's token is versioned: the server's statistics epoch and, for every relation `seed.json` names plus `page_projection_jobs`, its oid, relfilenode and `pg_stat_user_tables` insert, update and delete counts, plus the coverage-gap rows; a token of another shape is refused as `mark_unreadable`. The confirmation reads the same and refuses any difference (`stats_reset_changed`, `postmaster_restarted`, `relation_missing`, `relation_added`, `relation_oid_changed`, `relfilenode_changed`, `counter_decreased`, `writes_since_seal`), a new gap (`coverage_gap`) or a broken hook (`hook_missing`). Counters only show what the server has flushed, so at the seal and at the confirmation it also refuses while another backend holds a write open (`other_writer_open`), a prepared transaction exists (`prepared_transaction`), a recorded writer is still connected whatever its row's age (`other_writer_live`), another connection of a role that can write a copied relation (itself or through a role it can SET ROLE to) is not idle or went idle under a minute ago (`other_backend_recent`) or is hidden from the reading role (`other_backend_unseen`; a read-only role such as a monitoring agent is never waited on), or a login role can write a brain table with no way to record evidence (`uncovered_writer_role`); the cost is that a writer still connected during the seal check is refused until it commits or disconnects. It also refuses PostgreSQL older than 15 (`postgres_version_old`), `track_counts` off for the database or one of its roles (`track_counts_off`; a superuser's own `SET` is not visible) and a brain with no seed record (`seed_unreadable`). After the child took a confirmation the check reads once more and a failure sends the child `revokeSeal`, which invalidates the watermark with a full obligation and a new continuity; a confirmation sent but not acknowledged is revoked too (`seal_confirm_unacknowledged`), each revoke first leaves a `seal_revoked:<reason>` line the child reads on its own and is retried until acknowledged, and a doubt left over (`confirmSeal` returns it) makes the first check run a full pass and the close refuse to seal. PGLite never seals or confirms (`pglite_no_db_continuity`): nothing there proves no other process wrote between two sessions, so every session keeps its own full obligation until a pass. Seal lifecycle (`follow-child.ts`, `attach.ts`): taking a seal over removes it in the same first write that shows the watermark invalid; any later event removes it from disk before the next one; write capture stays on through the close, so a later before-disconnect write is recorded as lost; a lost-write line that cannot be written is retried (`lostWriteRecorder`) and blocks the seal (`lost_write_unrecorded`); a failed removal leaves a `seal_revoked` line that makes the seal stale, and a refused or failed-take start appends its blocker to the existing invalidation file and empties it only after its seal-free write. Every decision is `follower.json` `seal_outcome`, shown by status. Pinned by `test/shadow-seal-lifecycle.test.ts` and `test/e2e/shadow-seal-token.test.ts`. Any doubt leaves the watermark invalid until a full pass. The first reconcile check runs 5 s after start; a full pass saves `reconcile/progress.json` per interval, and a half pass a closed session left resumes under the same continuity, seed and epoch, with no failed pass recorded, unless the stopped part repaired rows or found misses: that part is recorded at once as a `stopped` full pass (the repair stays on record across a fresh pass) and makes the next full pass due. `follow.ts` `JOURNAL_BOOKKEEPING_MEMBERS` (an exact list) publishes what those journal members write instead of invalidating. Pinned by `test/shadow-follow-seal.test.ts`, `test/shadow-follow-units.test.ts`, `test/shadow-follow-producer.test.ts` and `test/e2e/shadow-serve-short-sessions.test.ts`.
