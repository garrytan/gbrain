- `src/core/search/keyword-or-rarity.ts` — rare words first in both engines' keyword OR retry. For at most `RARITY_MAX_KEYWORD_TERMS` (12) words (the title arm keeps `RARITY_MAX_TERMS` = 4: it runs one statement per word), `rareTermPagesSql` reads the pages of each word's first `RARE_TERM_CHUNK_CAP` + 1 visible chunks (brain-wide, weights only; stopwords and, on Postgres, words `commonOrTokens` in keyword-or-narrow.ts estimates past the cap are skipped); a fitting word weighs 1 / pages. `rankOrFallbackByRarity` re-runs the retry with the rarest words' OR and sorts by summed weight.
- `src/core/search/keyword-identifier-probe.ts` — #7491: `keywordArmRows` builds both engines' `searchKeyword` rows from the strict rows. With `orFallback`, offset 0 and no quote or negation, a query naming an identifier (`identifierTokens`: a hash number `#412`, or a token `identifier-query.ts` recognizes: a 6+ digit run, hex id, code, email, URL or phone) and other words runs the arm's own statement once more with the AND of the identifier tokens as the match query and the any-word query as the rank query: same scope, visibility, chunk predicate, filters and candidate cap as strict. Its rows are untagged (they vote like strict rows): after the strict rows when strict matched, ahead of the relaxed rows (`keyword_relaxed`) when it did not; one row per page, cut to the limit. A failed probe adds nothing. `holdsIdentifiers` lets the title arm's OR retry (`engine-sql/titles.ts`) lead with, untagged, the titles holding every identifier. Pinned by `test/search/identifier-probe.serial.test.ts` (+ Postgres E2E) and `test/search/keyword-identifier-probe.test.ts`.
- `src/core/search/keyword-handles.ts` — GBRA-108: `slugHandles` finds the query's slug handles (a hyphenated slug-leaf token with a letter, bare or quoted on its own, at most `MAX_SLUG_HANDLES` = 16; negated tokens and tokens inside a longer quoted phrase are not handles). They resolve by exact slug basename (`handleMatchSql`, served by `idx_pages_slug_basename` like `whois`) as an arm of the keyword statement (`search/keyword-statement.ts`) under its filters, scope and visibility, so `--types`, granted sources, private pages and quarantine apply. `match: "keyword"` lists and counts the named pages first; in `searchKeyword` (orFallback, offset 0) `keywordArmRows` puts them ahead of every other row, untagged. A query without a handle builds the statements it built before. Pinned by `test/search/slug-handles.serial.test.ts` (+ Postgres E2E) and `test/search/keyword-handles.test.ts`.
