I have a charger, but I do not know whether it works with AA rechargeables. The label says “NiMH AA/AAA,” followed by a model number. Is that enough?

[assistant]
That wording usually indicates the charger is intended for nickel-metal hydride AA and AAA rechargeable batteries. Before using it, check the charger’s full label or manual for:

- The permitted battery chemistry: it should say NiMH.
- The permitted sizes: AA is listed.
- The required input voltage and plug arrangement.
- Any instructions about charging times, indicator lights, or damaged cells.

Do not use it with disposable alkaline batteries. Also check the rechargeable batteries themselves: they should say NiMH and have a capacity rating in mAh. If the charger and cells both match those requirements, the charger is likely suitable, but follow the specific instructions printed on the charger.
