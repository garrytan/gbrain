[assistant]
Include enough information for the shop to identify the order and investigate, while avoiding unnecessary personal details. A useful message should contain:

- A polite greeting
- The order date or order number, if available
- The item description, stated generally
- The original estimated delivery date
- The latest tracking status and most recent scan date
- The fact that the address appears correct
- A request for them to check with the carrier or advise on next steps
- A request for an expected response timeframe, if convenient
- A neutral closing

You can leave out the full delivery address, payment information, and the complete tracking number unless the shop asks for them through its secure support channel.

What should I do if the shop replies that I need to contact the carrier myself?

[assistant]
You can contact the carrier, but first check whether the shop’s shipping terms say that the sender must open a trace or claim. Many carriers limit certain investigations or reimbursement requests to the sender, even if the recipient reports the problem.

If you contact the carrier, have these details ready:

- Tracking number
- Sender and destination postal codes
- Shipment date
- Latest scan date and location
- Original estimated delivery date
- A short description such as “No movement or delivery update since March 7”

Ask specifically:

1. Is the package currently moving, held, or awaiting a scan?
2. Is there an active delivery exception?
3. Can the carrier open a missing-package inquiry?
4. Does the sender need to initiate the inquiry?
5. What is the expected timeframe for the next update?

Record the case or reference number, if one is provided, and send that reference back to the shop through the same private support channel.
