What is the difference between “may be asked to move” and “will be asked to move”?

[assistant]
“May be asked to move” indicates a possibility. It does not say that every employee who parks in a visitor space will definitely be told to move.

“Will be asked to move” indicates an expected or definite action. It suggests that any employee who parks in a visitor space should expect a request to move the vehicle.

In this notice, “may” is softer but less firm. “Will” is clearer about enforcement but can sound stricter.
