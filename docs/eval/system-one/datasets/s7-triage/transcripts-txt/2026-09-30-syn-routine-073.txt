1. Press `Windows + R`.
2. Enter `services.msc`, then press Enter.
3. Find **Print Spooler** in the list.
4. Right-click it and select **Restart**.
5. Reopen **Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners**, open the queue, and check whether it is empty.

Use the following cleanup sequence. It removes the printer and any remaining spool files before reinstalling it.

1. Open **Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners**.
