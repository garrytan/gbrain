[assistant]
It is not grammatically wrong, but “must ensure” is strong and can sound more burdensome than necessary. It places responsibility on the employee to verify the vehicle’s status, rather than simply stating the parking condition.

Possible alternatives are:

- “Vehicles must display a current permit.”
- “A current permit must be displayed on each vehicle.”
- “Employees are responsible for ensuring that their vehicles display a current permit.”
- “Employees must display a current permit on their vehicles.”

For a formal notice, “Vehicles must display a current permit” is the most direct. If the policy specifically assigns responsibility to employees, use the third option.

Could you list the main grammar changes between my original notice and that recommended version?

[assistant]
The main changes are:

1. **“are reminded that parking is permitted” → “may park”**  
   The revision uses a direct modal verb. It is shorter and states the permitted action more clearly.

2. **“only in spaces allocated to them” → “only in their designated spaces”**  
   The possessive adjective avoids the longer relative phrase and removes the repeated wording.

3. **“Vehicles parked in spaces allocated to other employees” → “Vehicles parked elsewhere”**  
   “Elsewhere” is a concise adverb referring to locations other than the permitted spaces. It also avoids repeating “allocated.”

4. **“may be removed” remains unchanged**  
   This is already grammatical and appropriately formal. It leaves the consequence conditional rather than asserting that removal will always occur.

5. **“Employees must ensure that their vehicles display a current permit” → “All vehicles must display a current permit”**  
   The subject changes from employees to vehicles, making the requirement direct. This also avoids the potentially heavy construction “must ensure that.”

6. **“Any questions should be directed to Facilities” → “Questions should be directed to Facilities”**  
   Removing “Any” makes the sentence more concise without changing its meaning.

The revised notice is:

“Employees may park only in their designated spaces. Vehicles parked elsewhere may be removed. All vehicles must display a current permit. Questions should be directed to Facilities.”
