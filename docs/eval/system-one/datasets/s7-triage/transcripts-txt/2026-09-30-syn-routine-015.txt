Now I have paper towels. Pack A is 6 rolls, 110 sheets per roll, for $13.50. Pack B is 8 rolls, 90 sheets per roll, for $14.40. Pack C is 12 rolls, 80 sheets per roll, for $19.80. How should I compare them?

[assistant]
First calculate total sheets, then price per roll and price per 100 sheets:

| Pack | Total sheets | Price per roll | Price per 100 sheets |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 6 × 110 = 660 | $13.50 ÷ 6 = **$2.25** | $13.50 ÷ 660 × 100 = **$2.05** |
| B | 8 × 90 = 720 | $14.40 ÷ 8 = **$1.80** | $14.40 ÷ 720 × 100 = **$2.00** |
| C | 12 × 80 = 960 | 19.80 ÷ 12 = **$1.65** | $19.80 ÷ 960 × 100 = **$2.06** |

For sheet count alone, Pack B is the lowest at $2.00 per 100 sheets. Pack C has the lowest price per roll, but each roll has fewer sheets, so its per-100-sheet price is slightly higher than B’s.

[user]
Does sheet size matter here?

[assistantassistant]
Yes. Sheet count is only a fair comparison if the sheets are the same size. If one sheet is larger, it provides more towel area.

For a more complete comparison, record:

- Sheets per roll
- Number of rolls
- Sheet length
- Sheet width
- Total square feet or square meters
- Final package price

Calculate total area as:

`number of rolls × sheets per roll × sheet length × sheet width`

Keep the dimensions in the same unit. For inches:

`total square feet = rolls × sheets per roll × length in inches × width in inches ÷ 144`

Then calculate:

`price per square foot = package price ÷ total square feet`

Pack A is lowest under this comparison.

Can you give me a reusable spreadsheet formula for both categories?

[assistant]
Yes. Here is a simple spreadsheet layout that works in Excel or Google Sheets.

For olive oil, use these columns:

`A: Product`  
`B: Price`  
`C: Volume in fl oz`  
`D: Price per fl oz`  
`E: Price per 100 mL`

In `D2`:

`=B2/C2`

In `E2`:

`=B2/(C2*29.5735)*100`

Copy the formulas down for additional products.

For paper towels, use:

`A: Pack`  
`B: Price`  
`C: Rolls`  
`D: Sheets per roll`  
`E: Sheet length in inches`  
`F: Sheet width in inches`  
`G: Total sheets`  
`H: Price per 100 sheets`  
`I: Total square feet`  
`J: Price per square foot`

In `G2`:

`=C2*D2`

In `H2`:

`=B2/G2*100`

In `I2`:

`=G2*E2*F2/144`

In `J2`:

`=B2/I2`

Format the price columns as currency and the unit-price columns to two or three decimal places.
