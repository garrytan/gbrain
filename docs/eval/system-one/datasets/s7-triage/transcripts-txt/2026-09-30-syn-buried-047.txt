[assistant]
Use this checklist for each toy:

1. Turn the toy off before opening the compartment.
2. Check the compartment label for the required number and size; all five of yours say AA.
3. Match each battery’s plus and minus ends to the molded symbols or diagram. The flat end is negative; the raised button end is positive.
4. Make sure the spring usually contacts the flat negative end, but follow the compartment markings rather than relying only on that rule.
5. Install all batteries in the same compartment at the same time; do not mix new and used cells.
6. Close and secure the cover before testing.
7. Look for a separate power switch, especially on the car, dinosaur, game, and drum.
8. Test the toy briefly. If it does not work, turn it off and recheck orientation instead of inserting more batteries.
9. If a compartment has corrosion, leakage, a loose contact, or a damaged cover, do not force the batteries in.
10. Remove batteries from toys that will be stored for a long period.

Your quantities are 4, 3, 2, 4, and 3, so count each compartment before closing it.
