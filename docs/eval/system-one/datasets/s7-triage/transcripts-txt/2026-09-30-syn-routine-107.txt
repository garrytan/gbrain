When I paste that, the quotation marks and dashes look slightly different from the rest of my document. Can I replace them with straight quotes and regular hyphens?

[assistant]
You can, but MLA formatting normally uses typographic quotation marks and en dashes where appropriate. Straight quotation marks are readable but less polished, and regular hyphens should not replace en dashes in page ranges. If your document software changes them automatically, that is usually acceptable. To keep the formatting consistent, use one font throughout and let the word processor handle smart punctuation.

For page ranges, use en dashes:
- 44–58
- 101–15

For quoted titles, use double quotation marks:
- “Mulch Basics”
- “Rain Barrels for Beginners”

Do not add quotation marks around book or journal titles.

MLA alphabetizes by the first element of each entry, ignoring initial articles such as “A,” “An,” and “The” in titles. If two entries have the same author, organization, or first element, alphabetize those entries by title. Ignore an initial A, An, or The when alphabetizing the title. For example:

In a bibliography, repeat the author’s name for each entry unless your instructor specifically asks for a different convention.
