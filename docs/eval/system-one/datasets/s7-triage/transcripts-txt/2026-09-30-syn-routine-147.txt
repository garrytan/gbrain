Crack two large eggs into a small bowl, beat them just until the yolks and whites are combined, and weigh or measure the mixture. A large egg without its shell is typically about 50 g or roughly 45–50 mL, so one and a half eggs is approximately 75 g or 68–75 mL of beaten egg.

The most consistent method is:

1. Crack and beat two eggs.
2. Weigh the mixture.
3. Remove half of it, leaving approximately 75 g if the two eggs weigh about 150 g total.
4. Use the remaining 75 g in the batter.

If the eggs are unusually small or large, measure 1.5 times the weight of one egg rather than relying on a fixed number. Keep the unused portion refrigerated in a covered container and use it promptly in another cooked dish.

- 3/8 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract

Preparation:
1. Crack two large eggs into a bowl and beat until combined. Measure 75 g, or 5 tablespoons, for the recipe.
2. Heat the fan oven to 170°C. If it runs noticeably hot, use 165°C.
3. Line 18 standard muffin cups with paper liners.
4. Mix the dry ingredients separately. Combine the wet ingredients separately, then mix the two groups just until combined. Divide the batter evenly among the liners, filling each about two-thirds to three-quarters full.
5. Bake on the center rack. Do not open the oven during the first 17 minutes.
6. Check at 17 minutes with a toothpick. If it shows wet batter, bake for 2 more minutes and check again. Continue in 1-minute increments until the toothpick comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs. Expect approximately 18–21 minutes.
7. Cool in the tin for 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack.
