Compact recipe for 7 standard muffins:

Ingredients:
- 140 g plain flour
- 87.5 g caster sugar
- 4.7 g baking powder
- 1.75 g fine salt
- 66 g unsalted butter, melted and cooled slightly
- 58 g beaten egg
- 105 mL milk
- 2.9 mL vanilla extract
- 131 g blueberries, fresh or frozen

Method:
1. Heat the fan oven to 170°C. Line six cups of the dark tin.
2. Whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
3. In another bowl, whisk the measured egg, milk, vanilla, and melted butter.
4. Add the wet mixture to the dry mixture and fold just until no dry flour remains.
5. Fold in the blueberries gently.
6. Weigh the batter and divide it into seven equal portions. Refrigerate the seventh portion, covered, while the first six bake.
7. Bake six muffins on the center rack for 18–22 minutes. They are done when a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs.
8. Cool the tin briefly, then bake the final muffin at the same temperature, checking from 18 minutes.

If using frozen blueberries, fold them in straight from frozen and expect the batter to take slightly longer to come together, but do not increase the oven temperature.
