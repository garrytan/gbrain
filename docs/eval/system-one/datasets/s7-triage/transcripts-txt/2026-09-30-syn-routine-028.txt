A compatible wheel may not be selected for several routine reasons:

- Your `pip` may be old and unable to recognize newer wheel metadata.
- The package version selected may not publish a wheel for your platform or architecture.
- An environment setting such as `--no-binary`, `PIP_NO_BINARY`, or a requirements file may force source builds.
- The index or mirror you are using may contain only the source archive.
- The platform may be using an unusual architecture or Python build.
