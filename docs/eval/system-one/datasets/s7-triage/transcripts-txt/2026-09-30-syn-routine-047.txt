Before you format them, can you explain how MLA handles missing authors and dates? Also, should I include URLs, and do I need hanging indents?

[assistant]
For missing authors, begin with the title, alphabetizing by the first significant word. Do not use “Anonymous” unless that is explicitly given as the author.

For missing dates, omit the date rather than writing “n.d.” in a standard MLA 9 Works Cited entry. If your instructor or assignment sheet specifically asks for access dates, add “Accessed” followed by the day, month, and year.

URLs are commonly included for online sources. MLA usually omits the protocol prefix “https://,” although leaving it in is not generally a serious formatting problem if your teacher accepts clickable links. Remove a final period from a URL only when the punctuation could be mistaken as part of the address; otherwise, MLA entries end with a period.

Use double spacing throughout, with a hanging indent of 0.5 inch for each entry. In a word processor, select the bibliography, open Paragraph settings, choose a 0.5-inch hanging indent, and set line spacing to double. Alphabetize the complete list by author surname or by title when no author is available.
