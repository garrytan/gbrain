How should I handle the laundry if the hotel machine is shared? I might only have about an hour available, and I don’t want clothes sitting wet while I’m out.

[assistant]
Plan the laundry for a specific window rather than treating it as a spare-time task. A workable sequence is:

1. Ask the front desk when the machine is least busy and whether detergent is provided.
2. Start the wash only when you can return before the cycle ends.
3. Wash underwear, socks, and the most-used top; do not wait until checkout day.
4. Use the shortest appropriate cycle, then move everything immediately to the dryer.
5. Choose a low or medium heat setting and check the load after 20–30 minutes.
6. Remove items as soon as they are dry and hang them over hangers, the shower rail, or the back of a chair.

For an hour, keep the load small—roughly one compact packing cube, not a full hamper. Bring two detergent sheets, a zippered laundry bag, and a few clothespins. If the dryer is occupied or unreliable, hand-wash only the urgent items in the sink and roll them in a towel to remove excess water.
