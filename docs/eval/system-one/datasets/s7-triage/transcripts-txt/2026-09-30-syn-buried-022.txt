## Before Sunday

Shop on Saturday if possible. Refrigerate chicken and salmon immediately at 4°C / 40°F or below. Keep chicken on the lowest refrigerator shelf in a sealed tray. If either package will not be used within one to two days, freeze it and thaw it in the refrigerator, never on the counter.

- One large pot for lentil sauce

- One soup pot or clean container for soup

- Labels or masking tape

## Sunday prep order

### 1. Wash and organize produce — 15 minutes

Wash vegetables that will be eaten cooked or raw. Keep mushrooms dry until use if possible. Set aside:

- Roast vegetables
- Chicken-tray vegetables
- Broccoli
- Grain-salad cucumber and parsley
- Lentil-sauce vegetables

Use one cutting board for produce first. Do not put raw chicken on it until all ready-to-eat produce has been cut and the board has been washed with hot, soapy water.

### 2. Start the lentil sauce — 45–60 minutes

Use the large pot:

1. Sauté onion, carrot, celery, and mushrooms.
2. Add lentils, canned tomatoes, herbs, and water or stock.
3. Simmer until the lentils are tender.
4. Stir in spinach near the end.
5. Divide the sauce into shallow containers.

Do not cool the full pot on the counter or place a large hot pot directly into the refrigerator. Portion it into shallow containers, leave them uncovered briefly while steam subsides, then refrigerate within two hours of finishing. Once cold, cover them. Keep enough for Day 6 and Day 7 in the refrigerator if using within four days; freeze the Day 7 portion if your schedule could extend beyond that.

### 3. Cook rice and farro or barley — 30–45 minutes

Use the rice cooker for rice and the medium pot for farro or barley, or cook one after the other to avoid extra equipment.

- Cool cooked rice quickly in a shallow container.
- Refrigerate it within two hours.
- Use refrigerated rice within three to four days.
- For rice needed later in the week, freeze portions promptly once cooled and thaw them in the refrigerator.

For the grain salad, cool the farro or barley separately. Do not mix in cucumber or herbs until serving; this keeps the salad fresher.

### 4. Roast vegetables and chickpeas — 35–45 minutes

Roast the Day 1 vegetables and chickpeas on one or two sheet pans at 220°C / 425°F. Avoid crowding so they roast rather than steam.

Cool the batch in shallow containers. Reserve the Day 3 portion in its own labeled container. Refrigerate within two hours and use within three to four days.

You can reuse a sheet pan for the chicken after the vegetables are done, but wash it if raw chicken will touch it.

### 5. Prepare the chicken tray bake — 45–55 minutes

Use the second sheet pan if available. Put the vegetables on first, then add the chicken according to the recipe timing. The thickest part of the chicken should reach at least 74°C / 165°F.

After cooking:

- Keep the two Day 2 dinner portions separate.
- Remove the chicken from the bones or divide it into the portions intended for soup.
- Cool in shallow containers.
- Refrigerate within two hours.
- Use the cooked chicken within three to four days.

Because Day 4 soup is only a couple of days away, refrigeration is appropriate. If you may not eat it by then, freeze the reserved chicken promptly.

### 6. Do not cook the salmon on Sunday

Keep the salmon raw and refrigerated until the day before or day of the meal. Fish is best cooked close to serving. If you need to prepare it ahead, bake it no more than one day in advance, cool it quickly, and use it within one to two days.

### 7. Make the soup on Day 4

Use the reserved chicken and vegetables with stock, canned tomatoes, celery, and rice or pasta. Bring the soup fully to a simmer. Add rice or pasta only to the amount being eaten soon, because it absorbs liquid and becomes soft during storage.

Cool extra soup in shallow containers and refrigerate within two hours. Use it within three to four days, or freeze it.

## Suggested storage labels

Write the contents and date on each container:

- “Roast veg/chickpeas — Sun — use by Wed”
- “Grain — Sun — use by Wed”
- “Chicken — Sun — use by Wed”
- “Lentil sauce — Sun — use by Wed/Thu”

When reheating leftovers, heat them until steaming hot throughout, ideally to 74°C / 165°F. Discard food that sat at room temperature for more than two hours, or more than one hour in very hot conditions. Keep the refrigerator at or below 4°C / 40°F, and avoid stacking hot containers tightly together because that slows cooling.
