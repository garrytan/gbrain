[assistant]
Both are grammatically correct, but they differ slightly in tone and meaning:

- “Any vehicle left there could be removed” presents removal as a possible consequence. It sounds plain and conversational.
- “Any vehicle left there may be removed” is more formal and sounds like a standard notice. It can also suggest that removal is permitted or may occur.

For a routine office notice, “may be removed” is probably the better fit:

“Any vehicle left there may be removed.”

If you want the least formal wording, keep “could be removed.”
