I’d prefer something I can put in a phone reminder. Is every Wednesday and Sunday reasonable, or is that too often?

[assistant]
Twice-weekly reminders are reasonable as reminders to check, but not necessarily to water. Set them for Wednesday and Sunday with wording such as “Check soil; water only if top inch is dry.” That avoids watering while the mix is still damp.

If you want fewer reminders, use Sunday as the main check and Wednesday as a backup. During winter, change to once weekly. The plant’s actual water needs may vary with sunlight, indoor heating, and the potting mix.

What exactly should I do when the reminder goes off? I tend to either add a small splash or accidentally overdo it.

[assistant]
Use this repeatable process:

1. Push a finger or a wooden stick about 1 inch into the soil near the pot’s edge.
2. If it feels cool and damp, do not water. Check again at the next reminder.
3. If it feels dry and the stick comes out mostly clean, take the pot to a sink.
4. Pour room-temperature water slowly over the soil surface.
5. Stop when water begins to come through the drainage holes.
6. Let the pot drain for 10–15 minutes.
7. Empty any collected water from the saucer or outer container.
8. Return the plant to its usual spot.

Do not rely on a tiny splash, since it may wet only the surface and leave the lower soil dry.

[user]
How much water should I pour before I expect drainage? I’m worried that “water until it drains” could mean a lot for a small plant.

[assistant]
For a 6-inch pot, start with about 2 cups, poured slowly over 30–60 seconds. Wait a minute, then check the drainage holes. If nothing has drained and the soil still looks dry, add another half cup.

The exact amount depends on how dry and compacted the mix is. The goal is thorough wetting, not a fixed volume. Once a small amount drains, stop adding water and let it sit over the sink. If water rushes straight through immediately, the mix may be pulling away from the pot edges; pause for a minute and add smaller amounts more slowly.

[user]
The room gets noticeably drier when the heater runs, but I don’t want to change the plan every time the weather shifts. Is checking twice a week still a good default?

[assistant]
Yes. Keep the twice-weekly checks as your default during the brighter part of the year. When the heater is running, the soil may dry faster, so the checks are useful, but still water only when the top inch is dry.

You can add one simple adjustment rule:

- Dry at both checks for two weeks in a row: keep the schedule, but inspect the soil more carefully and consider checking one extra time between reminders.
- Damp at both checks for two weeks in a row: change to one check weekly until it begins drying normally.
- Alternating dry and damp: keep the twice-weekly schedule.

This avoids reacting to one unusually warm or cool day.
