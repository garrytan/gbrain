Can you compare “guidelines,” “rules,” “requirements,” and “restrictions” in this context? I want to choose the least severe word that still makes the notice clear.

[assistant]
Here is the practical difference:

- “Guidelines” suggests recommendations or general directions. It sounds friendly, but it may seem optional.
- “Rules” indicates that the instructions are mandatory. It is plain and easy to understand.
- “Requirements” emphasizes that people must meet specified conditions. It sounds more formal and administrative.
- “Restrictions” focuses on what is not allowed. It can sound firm, especially when referring to reserved or limited areas.

For a notice that needs to be clear without sounding severe, “parking guidelines” is the least forceful choice. If the instructions are mandatory, “parking rules” is clearer. You could write:

“Please follow these parking guidelines.”

Or, for a firmer but still ordinary version:

“Please follow these parking rules.”
