375°F converts to approximately 190.6°C, so set the oven to 190°C. If your oven uses gas marks, that is roughly Gas Mark 5.

The baking time usually does not multiply when the number of muffins increases, because each muffin is still the same size. Bake at 190°C for about 20–22 minutes, checking near the 20-minute mark. A batch of 30 may need to be divided between multiple trays or baked in rounds. I may not have enough muffin tins for all 30. If I bake them in two rounds, how should I handle the batter while the first round is in the oven?

[assistant]
For the most even result, mix the batter all at once, then portion it into the first available tray and keep the remaining batter covered while the first round bakes. Put the unbaked batter in the refrigerator if it will sit for more than about 10 minutes. Keep the blueberries mixed in gently, and avoid stirring the batter repeatedly because that can make the later muffins tougher.

A practical sequence is:

1. Line or grease the available muffin wells.
2. Mix the batter until the dry streaks just disappear.
3. Fold in the blueberries.
4. Fill the first tray.
5. Cover the remaining batter and refrigerate it.
6. Bake the first tray at 190°C for 20–22 minutes.
7. Let the tray cool enough to handle, then portion and bake the second round.

The second round may rise slightly differently because the leaveners begin reacting once the wet and dry ingredients are combined, but keeping the batter cool and disturbing it as little as possible helps.
