[assistant]
That usually means the parcel left a sorting facility but has not received its next arrival or delivery scan. A single missed day can happen because of a trailer delay, weather, a backlog, or a scan being recorded late. Since the estimate disappeared, I’d do three things:

1. Check the tracking page again later today and look for a service-alert banner.
2. Confirm that the delivery address and any delivery instructions shown in the order are correct.
3. Contact the seller or carrier if there is still no scan after one full business day, or sooner if the contents are time-sensitive.

Avoid posting the full tracking number publicly. If you contact support, include the order date, the last scan, the original estimate, and a request for the current status and next expected scan.

The chat bot is asking me to choose between “package delayed,” “package lost,” and “change delivery.” Which one fits?

[assistant]
Choose “package delayed.” The available information shows a missed estimate and no recent scan, but it does not establish that the package is lost. “Package lost” is usually for cases where the carrier has completed a search, marked it missing, or instructed you to file a claim. “Change delivery” is for altering delivery options, such as a pickup location or delivery date, rather than asking why the shipment has not progressed.
