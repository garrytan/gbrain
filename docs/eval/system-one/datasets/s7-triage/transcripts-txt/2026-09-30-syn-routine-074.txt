Why is “all vehicles need” correct, while “all vehicles needs” is not? Please explain briefly and give two similar examples.

[assistant]
“Vehicles” is plural, so it takes the plural form of the verb: “need.” The singular form “needs” is used with a singular subject such as “the vehicle.”

Examples:

- “All visitors need to use the main entrance.”  
  “The visitor needs to use the main entrance.”

- “These spaces need to remain clear.”  
  “This space needs to remain clear.”

The same pattern applies to other present-tense verbs: “vehicles are,” not “vehicles is,” and “vehicles have,” not “vehicles has.”
