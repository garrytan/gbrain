The paper towel packages don’t list sheet dimensions in my notes. Is price per sheet enough, or should I compare square footage?

[assistant]
Price per sheet is useful only when the sheets are the same size. A “sheet” can vary in length and width, so two packages with identical sheet counts may provide different total coverage.

For a stronger comparison, record:
- Number of rolls
- Sheets per roll
- Sheet length
- Sheet width
- Package price

Then calculate:

- Total sheets = rolls × sheets per roll
- Area per sheet = sheet length × sheet width
- Total square feet = total sheets × area per sheet ÷ 144, if dimensions are in inches
- Price per 100 square feet = package price ÷ total square feet × 100

For example, if a package has 660 sheets measuring 11 × 10 inches:

- Area per sheet = 110 square inches
- Total area = 660 × 110 ÷ 144 = 504.17 square feet
- Price per 100 square feet = $9.99 ÷ 504.17 × 100 = about $1.98
