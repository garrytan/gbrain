My inbox has about 18,000 messages, mostly newsletters and automated updates. I want to clean it up without accidentally deleting anything important. Can you help me make a safe plan?

[assistant]
Yes. Use a staged process: identify likely newsletters, label or archive them first, review the results, and only then delete older low-value mail. Avoid broad rules based only on words like “update” or “notification,” because those can catch account and security messages.

A practical sequence is:

1. Create labels such as `Newsletters`, `Receipts`, `Account alerts`, and `Review later`.
2. Search for common newsletter signals, but inspect the results before acting.
3. Apply a label and archive a small batch.
4. Check that important mail was not included.
5. Create narrow filters for recurring senders or recognizable list headers.
6. Delete only messages that are labeled and older than a chosen period.
7. Keep deleted messages in the trash until the normal automatic cleanup period.

Start with a search-only pass. Do not select all results yet. For Gmail-style search, examples include:

```text
has:unsubscribe older_than:30d
category:promotions older_than:30d
-list: alerts newer_than:90d
```

The exact search syntax varies by service, so tell me which email provider or app you use if you want filter instructions that match its menus.

- Whether the sender address is consistent.

- Whether there are attachments, receipts, confirmations, or account-specific details.

For a cautious first pass, you could search:

```text
has:unsubscribe older_than:60d -is:starred
```

Do not exclude starred mail as a complete safety check, but it reduces the chance of touching messages you deliberately marked. Also consider excluding categories or labels you already use for receipts and account mail.

from:(newsletter@example.invalid) subject:("Weekly digest") older_than:60d

Do not use only `older_than:1y` because that would include unrelated mail. Also note that deleting a conversation can move all messages in that conversation to Trash, so inspect whether newsletters are grouped with anything else before confirming.
