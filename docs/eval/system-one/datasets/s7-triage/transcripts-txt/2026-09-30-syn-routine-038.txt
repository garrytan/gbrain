The 5 GHz connection still dropped once at the desk after about ten minutes. The 2.4 GHz connection has stayed steady. How do I change the channel?

[assistant]
You’ll change it in the router’s administration page. The exact labels vary, but the process is usually:

1. Connect to the router, preferably on 2.4 GHz or with an Ethernet cable.
2. Open a browser and enter the router address. Common addresses are:
   - `192.168.0.1`
   - `192.168.1.1`
   - `10.0.0.1`
3. Sign in using the router’s admin credentials. These may be printed on its label or shown in the setup documentation.
4. Look for **Wi-Fi**, **Wireless**, or **Advanced wireless settings**.
5. Select the **5 GHz** band.
6. Find **Channel** or **Control channel**.
7. If it is set to **Auto**, choose a fixed non-DFS channel such as 36, 40, 44, or 48.
8. Save or apply the change. The 5 GHz network may disappear briefly.
9. Reconnect the laptop and test from the desk.

Do not change the 2.4 GHz channel yet. Also leave channel width on Auto for the first test. If the page offers separate settings for “channel” and “channel width,” change only the channel.
