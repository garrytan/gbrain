[assistant]
Save a copy of the submitted message or take a screenshot showing the submission confirmation. Then:

- Note the date and time you contacted the seller.
- Keep checking the tracking page once or twice a day rather than repeatedly.
- Watch for email replies, including a junk or promotions folder.
- Keep the order confirmation and tracking details together.
- If the carrier offers delivery notifications, verify that they are enabled.
- Avoid opening duplicate cases unless the seller’s form indicates that the first submission failed.

If the seller gives a response-time estimate, use that as the point for a follow-up. If no estimate is provided, waiting one business day before following up is a reasonable routine approach.
