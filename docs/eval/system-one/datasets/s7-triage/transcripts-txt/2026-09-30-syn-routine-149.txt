[assistant]
For a standalone online video, put the video title in quotation marks, then list the uploader or channel, the upload date, and the URL. For example:

“Reading a Weather Map.” Northside Learning, 12 Sept. 2021, https://video.example/watch?v=WX204.

You do not need an access date for every URL in MLA 9. Add one when the page has no publication or update date, or when the content is likely to change over time and the access date helps document what you consulted. For your list, the website article, video, and journal article already have dates, so access dates are optional rather than required. If your teacher has a separate handout rule, follow that rule consistently.

1. Select the four entries, not the “Works Cited” heading.
2. Open the paragraph-formatting settings.
3. Find “Indentation” or “Special indent.”
4. Choose “Hanging.”
5. Set the hanging indent to 0.5 inch, or about 1.27 cm.
6. Set line spacing to double if your class requires standard MLA layout.
7. Keep the heading centered, with no hanging indent.

Do not create the hanging indent by inserting spaces, because the alignment can change when the font or page width changes.

I noticed the book title and article titles are in title case. Is that definitely what MLA wants, and what about the URL punctuation?

[assistant]
Yes. For English-language MLA entries, use headline-style capitalization for titles: capitalize the first word, the last word, and the principal words, including nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, and subordinating conjunctions. Keep short articles, coordinating conjunctions, and prepositions lowercase unless they are the first or last word.

Your titles therefore appear as:

- The Practical Science of Weather
- “Why Clouds Form”
- “Reading a Weather Map”
- “Rainfall Patterns in Small Watersheds”

For URLs, place a period after the URL when it ends the entry. The period is citation punctuation, not part of the web address. If a URL contains punctuation or wraps onto another line, do not add a manual hyphen. Let the word processor wrap it automatically. MLA also recommends using a DOI in the https://doi.org/ format when a DOI is supplied.
