Before I do it, what exactly will be lost? I want to avoid being surprised by something like the Wi-Fi devices no longer connecting or the Internet needing a special setting.

[assistant]
A factory reset returns the router to its out-of-box configuration. Commonly lost items include:

- Administrator username/password changes and any custom recovery settings
- Wi-Fi network name, Wi-Fi password, guest network settings, and wireless schedules
- Internet/WAN settings, including static addressing, VLAN settings, or PPPoE credentials if they were configured
- LAN address changes, DHCP reservations, custom DNS servers, and local host names
- Port-forwarding, port-triggering, UPnP-related preferences, firewall rules, VPN settings, and remote-management settings
- Parental controls, access-control lists, traffic limits, logs, and firmware-specific options

Your phones, laptops, televisions, and other devices will not be erased, but they will stop connecting automatically if the reset changes the Wi-Fi name or password. You can configure the router afterward with the same Wi-Fi name and password, which may let many devices reconnect without editing each device. That does not restore advanced settings.

If your Internet service uses ordinary automatic addressing, reconnecting the modem may be enough. If it uses PPPoE, a static address, VLAN tagging, or provider-specific activation, you will need those details before reconnecting.
