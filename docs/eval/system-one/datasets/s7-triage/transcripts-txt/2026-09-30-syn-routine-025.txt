Can you make the laundry plan more concrete? Assume I arrive around 6 p.m. on the first day, leave around 4 p.m. on the third day, and the machine is available from 8 p.m. on the second day. I’d prefer not to do hand-washing unless something goes wrong.

[assistant]
A simple schedule would be:

First day:
- Wear the travel outfit.
- Keep the first packed socks and underwear available as a fresh change if needed.
- Put worn items in the laundry bag rather than mixing them with clean clothes.

Second day:
- Start in a clean pair of socks and underwear.
- Wear a casual top for walking or work.
- Keep the nicer top clean for dinner or another setting where you want it.
- After changing in the evening, separate machine-washable items.

At 8 p.m. on the second day:
1. Load the worn socks, worn underwear, and casual tops.
2. Add the detergent sheet. If the machine is small, split the load only if necessary.
3. Use a normal or quick cycle according to the machine instructions.
4. Move everything to the dryer if the labels permit. Use low heat for stretch fabrics.
5. Remove items promptly when dry and keep the nicer top, trousers, sweater, and rain shell out of the load.
6. Put the clean items back in the packing cube.

Third day:
- Wear the freshly washed socks and underwear.
- Choose either a clean casual top or the nicer top.
- Keep one clean pair of socks and underwear available for the trip home.
- Leave the remaining damp items out to finish drying rather than packing them wet.

Because you have four pairs of socks and underwear, you can complete the trip even if the wash starts later than expected.
