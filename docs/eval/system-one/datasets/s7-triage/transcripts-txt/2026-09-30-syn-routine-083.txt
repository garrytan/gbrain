[assistant]
A few routine checks can reduce repeat problems:

- Keep the phone’s Bluetooth connection off while troubleshooting or until you finish using the headphones with the laptop.
- In Settings > System > Sound, confirm Headphones Stereo is the selected output when the headphones are connected.
- In Volume mixer, leave applications on Default unless one needs a separate output.
- Avoid choosing Headset Hands-Free unless you need the headphone microphone; Stereo generally provides better playback quality.
- Run Windows Update, including Optional updates only if they specifically mention Bluetooth, wireless, or audio.
- In Device Manager, open Intel(R) Wireless Bluetooth(R) > Properties > Power Management and see whether “Allow the computer to turn off this device to save power” is enabled. If it is, you can clear it and select OK.

Do not change unrelated adapter settings.

It is better not to remove it from Device Manager. The Hands-Free option is the Bluetooth headset profile, and Windows may need it for the headphone microphone or call applications. Removing profile-related entries can create another pairing problem.

You can also choose the laptop’s built-in microphone as the input device when you do not need the headphone microphone.
