Would it help to call, or is an online form better? I don’t want to spend a long time waiting on hold if the form is enough.

[assistant]
For a delay of about one day, an online form or chat is usually a reasonable first step, especially if the tracking page offers a “Where is my package?” or “Help with delivery” option. It creates a written record and lets you include the scan history.

Calling can be more useful when:

- The package is marked delivered but you do not have it.
- The address appears incorrect.
- The contents are time-sensitive.
- The tracking has stopped updating for several business days.
- The online form rejects the tracking number or gives only generic instructions.

You could submit the form today and call only if the response is automated, the case cannot be opened, or there is still no movement after another business day.

Before I submit it, should I ask them to open an investigation, or is that too strong after only one day?

[assistant]
After only one day, “please confirm the latest scan” is generally a softer and more appropriate request than asking for a formal investigation. Some carriers use “package inquiry” for ordinary delays, while “investigation” may be reserved for packages that are presumed lost or have had no movement for a longer period.

You could add a conditional sentence without sounding forceful:

“If the package cannot be located from the available scans, please let me know whether a package inquiry can be opened.”

That asks about the process while leaving room for the support team to apply its normal timing rules.

If it says “Out for delivery” tomorrow but nothing arrives, when should I contact them again?

[assistant]
If it changes to “Out for delivery” and the package is not delivered by the end of the carrier’s normal delivery window, first check for a delivery attempt notice or an updated scan. Sometimes an “Out for delivery” status remains until the route closes.

If there is still no delivery or explanation by the next morning, reply to the existing support thread or cite reference SR-20481. You can write:

“Hello, the package was marked ‘Out for delivery’ on Mar 17 but was not delivered, and there is no delivery attempt or exception shown. Could you please check the current status and advise on the next step? Thank you.”

If the status changes to “Delivered” but you cannot find the package, contact support promptly and check common safe-delivery locations first.
