[assistant]
Good question. Australia typically ends daylight saving in early April, while the US does not fall back until early November. Conversely, Australia starts daylight saving in early October, but the US springs forward in mid-March. So between mid-March and early April, both regions are on daylight saving and the offset is back to normal. Then from early April until early October, Australia is on standard time while the US is still on daylight time, shrinking the gap by one hour. It flips again in October. Also, just to confirm on the oven setting, one eighty c is three fifty five f, close enough to three fifty for any standard home oven without precise temperature control.
