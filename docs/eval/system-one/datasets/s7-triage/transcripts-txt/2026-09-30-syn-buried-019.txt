Can you explain exactly how the page should look in a Word document? I’m making a one-page handout, so I need practical settings, not just citation rules.

[assistant]
For an MLA Works Cited page, use these Word settings:

- Font: a readable font such as Times New Roman, 12 pt, unless your teacher specifies another.
- Margins: 1 inch on all sides.
- Line spacing: double throughout, including between entries.
- Alignment: left aligned, not justified.
- Heading: type `Works Cited` centered at the top; do not bold, underline, or put it in quotation marks.
- Entries: alphabetize them and apply a 0.5-inch hanging indent.
- Page number: place your last name and the page number in the upper-right header if your class requires MLA page headers.

To make the hanging indent in Word, select the entries, open Paragraph settings, choose “Special: Hanging,” and set it to 0.5 inch. Do not create the indent by pressing Tab at the beginning of each wrapped line.
