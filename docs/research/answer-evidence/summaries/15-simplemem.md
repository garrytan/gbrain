# 15 — SimpleMem: Efficient Lifelong Memory for LLM Agents

**Source:** [Primary publication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02553v3); arXiv v3 (preprint; latest archived revision as of research date).

SimpleMem combines structured ingestion, synthesis and retrieval planning. Its component-removal experiments are more useful for choosing hypotheses than its overall ranking. The reported answer metrics do not isolate retrieval recall or preprocessing cost. For GBrain, transfer one component at a time with fixed comparison conditions and an untouched decision set.
