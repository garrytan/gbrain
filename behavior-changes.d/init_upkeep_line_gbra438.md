The first-run settings block printed by `gbrain init` and `gbrain setup <harness>` (and its `--json` decisions) now includes an `upkeep` decision when it shows other settings: upkeep runs while an agent has `gbrain serve` open and idle, on by default, turned off with `gbrain config set maintenance.auto false`. New commands: `gbrain maintain --schedule` (opt-in launchd, systemd or cron job every 6 hours, preview with `--dry-run`, asks consent) and `--unschedule`.
