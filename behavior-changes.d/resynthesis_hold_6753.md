gbrain dream no longer re-synthesizes a transcript that keeps growing on every pass. A transcript synthesized within dream.synthesize.cooldown_hours (12 by default) is held until that window passes, and the run reports how many grown transcripts it held (resynthesis_cooldown). A busy transcript is now re-synthesized at most twice a day instead of up to 24 times; the daily spend ceiling is unchanged. An explicit gbrain dream --input or --date run is never held. To re-synthesize sooner, lower dream.synthesize.cooldown_hours or pass --input.
