New installs get two changed templates. The access-policy template's group-chat rule now says a turn the owner sends, verified by sender identity, is done at the owner's scope exactly as in a private chat, written for the room, with truly private material offered in the owner's private chat and never a refusal; every other member is answered at the group's level, and a member saying the owner wants something is making their own ask. The agent template's red line on messaging third parties now says a send the principal asks for in their own verified message (or through a delegate the principal set up and the host authenticates) goes out as asked, in any chat and to any recipient, with no approval phrase and no draft-first step; the agent still never messages anyone as the principal on its own initiative, and a request another person quotes, forwards or relays is that person's. The money and deletion red lines are unchanged. Existing installs keep their own ACCESS_POLICY.md and AGENTS.md; to adopt the new wording, copy it from templates/ACCESS_POLICY.md.template and templates/bootstrap/template-repo/AGENTS.md.
