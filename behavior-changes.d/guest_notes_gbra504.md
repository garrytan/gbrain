New guest notes (GBRA-504): a brain can keep what people outside it say in a group chat with your agent, in a guest source that only a listed broker credential writes, once per note. Nothing changes until you run gbrain guests init: a brain without a guest source reads, writes and costs exactly as before, and no grant, privacy label or search scope changes on upgrade. With a guest source, every read that returns a guest note marks it as an outside guest's words (origin guest, trust tier external_untrusted, a guest object in JSON and a warning line in text), guest notes feed no facts, takes, timeline entries or links, and your own CLI cannot write there. You keep a note as your own page with gbrain guests promote <slug> (MCP promote_guest_note, owner only; anyone else is refused guest_promotion_owner_only), after which the broker can no longer delete it (guest_page_promoted). A guest writer can delete only the guest notes it saved itself. Your own tokens and OAuth clients read the guest source only once it is on their read list: gbrain guests grant-owner-clients previews that and --yes applies it, and gbrain doctor --only guest_source checks the whole setup.
