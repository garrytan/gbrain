An open `gbrain serve` (stdio or HTTP) now runs the free unattended upkeep (`gbrain maintain --unattended`: link and timeline extraction, stale-page checks, free repair lanes, bookkeeping GC, and on a keyed brain the capped embedding step) while it is idle, at most once every 24 hours per brain, in slices of about 3 seconds that stop when a tool call arrives. It never deletes a page. `maintenance.auto false` or `GBRAIN_SWEEP=0` turns it off; an autopilot brain is left to autopilot. The record it writes is `maintenance.last_run` with runner `serve`.
