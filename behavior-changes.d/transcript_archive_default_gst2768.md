The conversation archive is now on by default on a personal brain: the maintenance pass (the idle upkeep of an agent's gbrain serve, gbrain maintain --unattended or its schedule, and autopilot) writes one redacted page per conversation per local day from your Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw, Hermes and Grok sessions, plus a day index, into the conversations source, then fills in older days a capped run at a time. On a keyless brain nothing leaves this machine. With an embedding key, new days are embedded by that provider under the existing daily embedding cap; older history is written keyword-only and its embedding waits until you see the price with gbrain embed --held --dry-run and approve it. No facts are extracted and no Dream synthesis runs on archive pages. To turn it off: gbrain transcripts archive off (pages already written stay). A shared team brain is not changed: it is only offered the archive, and stays off until its owner runs gbrain transcripts archive on. gbrain doctor --only transcript_archive_freshness says whether the archive is keeping up.
