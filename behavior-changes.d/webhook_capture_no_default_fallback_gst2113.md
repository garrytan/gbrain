A webhook capture (`POST /ingest` and its `ingest_capture` job) whose credential's write source is archived, unregistered or removed before the capture lands now fails the job with `source_changed` (`detail: webhook_source_unavailable`) and writes nothing. Before, it was written under `default`, a source the credential may not be granted, and could replace a page there. Restore the source and run `gbrain jobs retry` on the job. Captures that do not come from a webhook keep the `default` fallback.
