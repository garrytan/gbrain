Google contact linking now counts copies of one person page held in several sources as one person. When the copies share the slug, the matched email or phone and an agreeing name, the contact links to the default source's copy instead of waiting for a decision. Copies that disagree, or that you marked as different people, still wait. Decide the rest with gbrain dedupe decide; gbrain dedupe undo reverses any choice.
