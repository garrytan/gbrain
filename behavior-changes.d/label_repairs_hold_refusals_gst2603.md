gbrain repair visibility and gbrain repair private-labels now ignore stamps gbrain keeps only in the database (a pack-renamed type, the validate: false grandfather stamp, a legacy_type retype record) when they edit a page's label line, so those pages are labelled. A page whose file still differs in a real key, or whose label line cannot be edited as one line, is held by the preview with the blocking key and its next step and is left out of the approved set. A page that starts refusing after the preview is declined with label_edit_refused, nothing is written for it, the run exits 0 and it no longer counts toward item_failures_systemic. Their previews, and those of gbrain access rule add and import, also list same-slug pages in other sources that the change leaves readable (open_twins), with their count and the command that covers them; nothing changes those pages for you.
