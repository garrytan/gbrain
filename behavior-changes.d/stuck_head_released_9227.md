On a PGLite brain, the next gbrain process that opens the brain now releases at once every write claim an earlier, exited process left running and runs those writes again, instead of leaving later writes to the same source pending for the rest of the claim's lease (30 s, or about 6 minutes while publishing). `gbrain sources writer status` reports a claim whose owner has provably exited (that case on PGLite, a dead process on the same machine on Postgres) with `owner_exited: true`, `resumes_on_its_own: true`, `owner_live: false` and `next.code: claim_owner_exited`, where it said `claim_running`.
