gbrain access who-can-read and the open-page count (gbrain access serves, doctor open_shared_pages, the access serves several preview and the post-upgrade notice) now report the readers that get_page and search really serve on a brain that has, or once had, an owner-only source (an iMessage or Apple Contacts source, or a source marked owner_only). Before, once such a source existed, who-can-read said no member reads a page that a member does read (and suggested rewriting its readers), and the open-page count said every page names its readers. Reads themselves were always right. who-can-read now also lists only the owner for a page in, or derived from, an owner-only source, where it used to say every caller entitled to the source reads it.
