`gbrain config set cycle.grade_takes.auto_resolve.enabled true` now does what it documents (#7542): the dream cycle's `grade_takes` phase applies a verdict with confidence 0.95 or higher (3/3 at 0.85 or higher on the ensemble path) to the take. Before, the key was ignored and every verdict stayed review-only. Unset or false is unchanged: verdicts wait for review in `gbrain takes grades`.
