A managed connector sync (Google, GitHub, Slack, meetings and the others) run as a sync job, with the job drain on (the default), now stops at the job's own deadline, less its reserve, instead of running until the job is killed at its timeout. The run ends partial (reason timeout), records its pending writes and counts, and saves the newest position whose writes have already committed, so the job completes and the next sync picks up from there; before, every such job ended aborted: timeout, used up its attempts and saved nothing it had not saved mid-run. A connector whose recorded last failure is recovery_required no longer shows as stopped once no request of the source still holds a recovery record. sources status and sync status show recovery_settled, and the next sync that ends without an error clears the record. A real retained publication still keeps the stop. The recovery_required refusal now names the request, its state and blocked_reason, and its fix is gbrain write-request -- <request id>; its wait for that publication never runs past the caller's deadline. gbrain sources writer status now also lists a request that has ended but still holds a recovery record. Nothing to run after upgrading.
