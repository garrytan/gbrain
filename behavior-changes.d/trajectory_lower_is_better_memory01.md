gbrain eval trajectory, gbrain founder scorecard and the find_trajectory tool now know that for some metrics lower is better. For burn_rate, churn_rate, cac, burn, churn and any metric with the whole word cost, expense, loss, latency, payback or debt (or costs, expenses, losses, debts) unless the name also has saving, savings, saved, recovered, recovery, reduction, reduced or avoided (so cost_savings and revenue_loss_recovered stay ordinary metrics), a drop is no longer a regression or red flag; a rise of 10% or more is, and the scorecard says "burn rose 33.3%". Every other metric still flags a 10% drop. Each regressions entry gains an optional direction field (down or up); schema_version stays 1. The scorecard's growth delta also divides by the absolute prior value, so a loss shrinking from -100000 to -50000 reads up.
