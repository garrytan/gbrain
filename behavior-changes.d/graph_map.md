New command foresights graph-map shows a brain as one graph of pages and concepts. Each link is labelled stated (someone wrote it), inferred (the brain worked it out) or tool output (an outside tool reported it over MCP); clusters are found across the whole source; foresights graph-map export writes one self-contained HTML map that makes no network requests. Concept pages from the dream cycle are joined straight to the pages they come from. Private and deleted pages, and links touching them, are never read. It makes no AI calls.
