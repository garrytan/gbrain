New: when you correct your agent, the correct tool (gbrain correct on the command line) saves your words as a standing rule and replaces or withdraws the wrong fact in one call, with a receipt that says how far it got and what comes next; gbrain correct resume <receipt> finishes one that stopped part way, without writing anything twice. A rule from your own agent is searchable at once but reaches your agents' standing rules only after you approve that exact revision with gbrain rules approve <id>@<revision>; a rule from another member's agent stays proposed, and its fact unchanged, until you approve it. Guests, tool output and instruction-like rules are refused, and remote writes to rules/ pages outside correct are refused. Standing rules appear in context_pack with include_rules: true and in gbrain compile-context --include-rules; the session-start section is off until you run gbrain config set rules.session_start on. gbrain rules list, show, retire and check manage them, and gbrain doctor's new rules_regression check runs gbrain rules check.
