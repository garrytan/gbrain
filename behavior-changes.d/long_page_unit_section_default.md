search and query now widen a hit on a long page to its matching section by default. When a search hit lands on a long non-conversation page (a rendered body of at least search.long_page_min_chars characters, 20,000 by default), the result carries the section around the ranked passage instead of only that passage, as far as the search.return_budget_conversation budget and the long-page limits allow; such rows report delivered {unit: "section", reason: "long_page"}, and a section that does not fit keeps its ranked passage. Widened sections share that budget with conversation hits, so a result that holds both can carry less conversation text than before. Short pages, an explicit return_unit, a non-auto search.return_unit, an explicit token_budget, recall, think and assemble_evidence are unchanged. A brain that already set search.long_page_unit keeps its value. To keep the old behavior (every non-conversation hit its ranked passage), run: gbrain config set search.long_page_unit off
