The opt-in high-entropy assignment detector now also catches a credential whose key quote is escaped (`{\"password\":\"…\"}`, JSON inside a JSON string, with up to three backslashes) and one after a URL-encoded separator (`password%3D…`, `token%3A…`). The value stops before an escaped closing quote and, after an encoded separator, before the next `%26` parameter; a URL-encoded URL value is treated as a reference, not a secret. `gbrain doctor` asks once for a fresh `gbrain transcripts audit-secrets` run: it is read-only, makes no model or network call, and on a 5 MB synthetic transcript took about a third of a second. Transcripts imported from now on redact both shapes. Pages imported before are not rewritten; the audit lists the ones that hold such a line as affected.
