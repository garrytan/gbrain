The query facts block now shows a later retraction next to the fact it answers, for every caller. When the block shows a fact about a person or company, a later fact about the same entity that retracts it or calls it off (retracted, scratch that, I was wrong, cancelled, called off, fell through, not happening, never happened, no longer planned, just a rumour and similar) comes right after it, with its own date header, inside the block's usual share of the token budget and row count. It pairs only when it is dated after the fact, its cue is not negated ("not cancelled", "wasn't called off" and "isn't a rumour" never pair), and it carries the same slot (the same dimension and value) or, leaving out the entity's name and the cue words, every word it has is in the fact or it shares at least two words with it. It is read under the caller's read policy, so a remote caller or another member never sees a retraction it could not already read, and a forgotten or withdrawn fact never appears. There is no setting; a question with no such later fact gets exactly the block it got before.
