New: gbrain propose (MCP propose_jobs) lists jobs your agent could take on, found in data already in the brain with no model or provider call: people waiting on you, email threads you owe a reply, recurring meetings with no prep page, bulk senders you never wrote to, and the same question asked in two agents. Each job gives a count, the pages it found, and the installed skill or gbrain command that does it. gbrain propose accept or decline <signal> (MCP propose_decide) records your answer. On a new personal brain, once a Google source has synced, the first-session suggestion also offers the top job you have not answered; gbrain onboarding off turns that off with the rest of the suggestion. Only the install's owner can read or answer proposals.
