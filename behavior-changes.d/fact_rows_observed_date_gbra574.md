Saved facts in query results now say when they were said. The facts block (on by default for a query with a token budget), the temporal fact reserve, the facts arm and recall's date headers fill the observed date in their [observed ...; valid ...] header from the page the fact was taken from, read under the caller's own page access, so a page the caller may not read dates nothing. If that read fails the header shows observed unknown and the response reports the degraded stage fact_observed_date_unavailable (recall: facts_degraded reason fact_observed_date_unavailable). Copies of one claim take one row in the facts block, dated as first stated and followed by (stated N times, last DATE); facts that differ by even one word keep their own rows. Question ranking of saved facts now matches words on their stems, with a word matched only through its stem ranking a little below an exact match (a question naming Roger puts a Roger fact above a Rogers fact), and dates, years and auxiliary verbs such as had or been no longer count toward the match, so a question phrased as of a date admits the facts it names and a fact sharing only a year with the question is no longer admitted. A fact about another person who shares the named person's surname can still be admitted when it also shares a verb with the question; its row names that person.
