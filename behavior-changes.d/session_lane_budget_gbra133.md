New read-only `gbrain doctor` check `session_lane_budget` counts this database role's connections across every gbrain process (by process kind and host, from the `gbrain <kind>:<pid>:<nonce>` connection name) and warns at 80% of the limit, naming the kinds holding the most. Behind a Supabase pooler, set `GBRAIN_SESSION_LANE_LIMIT` to the project's `pool_size` to compare (Postgres cannot read it); without it the check reports the counts only. Without a pooler the limit is `max_connections` less the reserved slots. Not applicable on PGLite.
