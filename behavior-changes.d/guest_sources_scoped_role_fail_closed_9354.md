Brains whose gbrain process connects to Postgres as a scoped row-level-security role now need that role to read the guest_sources table. Before, such a role read it as empty, so guest-source checks passed every write as if no source were a guest source. Now a role that row-level security keeps from reading guest_sources is refused with writer_coordinator_required (unreadable_state, state guest_sources) on page, fact, take and timeline writes, because it cannot tell a guest source from an ordinary one. The table owner, a role that bypasses row-level security, and PGLite brains are unaffected. To keep writing, give the scoped writer the SELECT policy on guest_sources from the maintainer recipe in docs/ENGINES.md (derived-state maintainers).
