New command: gbrain identity shows who gbrain takes the brain's owner to be, worked out from what the brain already stores: the owner member's email, the account of each Google source the owner owns, and the owner's linked chat ids. A name never counts, and an identifier two members both claim counts for neither. It also names the page facts about the owner belong on. gbrain doctor gains an owner_identity check that warns when an identifier is shared between members, when several person pages carry an owner email, or when a Google source on a brain with several people is bound to no member. Nothing changes how facts are written yet.
