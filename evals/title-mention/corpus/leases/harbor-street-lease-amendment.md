---
title: "Harbor Street Lease Amendment"
type: note
---

This amendment modifies the original Harbor Street lease signed in 2021 between Acme Example Co and Northgate Holdings Example. Effective January 1, 2024, base rent increases to $19,900 per month and the term is extended by two years, ending February 28, 2028. The amendment supersedes the renewal option in the original lease, replacing it with a single two-year option at market rent. Parking is expanded from two spaces to four at $150 per space monthly. The tenant's share of operating expenses remains 14.2 percent, but a cap of five percent applies to annual increases in controllable expenses. The landlord agrees to fund a $60,000 improvement allowance for repainting and new carpet on the third floor, payable after completion and receipt of invoices. All other terms of the original remain in force. Signed by Bob Sample for the landlord and Alice Example for the tenant, with Carol Placeholder as witness.
