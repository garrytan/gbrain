This is the master lease for Suite 300 at 12 Maple Court, covering 8,200 rentable square feet leased by Acme Example Co from Linden Property Example. The term is seven years beginning July 1, 2022, with base rent of $27.50 per square foot annually, increasing three percent each July. Acme Example Co pays a security deposit of $45,000 and its pro rata share of taxes, insurance, and common area charges. Permitted use is office and training rooms only. The landlord provides eight reserved parking stalls and after-hours access through a key card system. Assignment or subletting requires landlord written consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld, and the landlord may recapture space in certain cases. The tenant must restore alterations at expiry unless waived in writing. Contact for lease administration is Dana Placeholder at Linden Property Example. Any partial subletting by the tenant is documented separately and remains subject to this master lease.
