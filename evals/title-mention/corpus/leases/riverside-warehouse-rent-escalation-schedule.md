title: "Riverside Warehouse Rent Escalation Schedule"
type: note
---

This schedule, attached as Exhibit B to the renewed Dock Road lease, lists monthly base rent for each year of the five-year term starting January 1, 2025. Year one is $21,333. Years two through five increase by a fixed 3.25 percent each January, producing $22,026, $22,742, $23,481, and $24,244 respectively. The figures apply to 32,000 square feet of warehouse space and 2,400 square feet of office. Additional rent for taxes and common charges is not included and is billed monthly on an estimated basis, then reconciled each March. If the consumer price index rises more than six percent in any calendar year, the landlord, Tidewater Realty Example, may request a review, but the fixed schedule governs unless both parties sign a written change. A rent abatement of half of month one applies only if the dock repairs finish on time. Prepared by Grace Placeholder in Finance and reviewed by Frank Example for lease compliance.
