Under this sublease, Acme Example Co, as sublandlord, sublets 2,600 square feet of Suite 300 at 12 Maple Court to Brightside Studio Example, as subtenant. The sublease term runs from October 1, 2023 through June 30, 2027, ending one day before the master lease expires. Subtenant rent is $29.00 per square foot annually, slightly above the rate Acme Example Co pays the master landlord, Linden Property Example. The subtenant deposits $12,000 and receives two reserved parking stalls and shared use of the conference room on a booking basis. The subtenant must follow every obligation in the master lease, carry its own liability coverage, and name both Acme Example Co and the master landlord as additional insureds. Linden Property Example gave written consent on September 12, 2023, signed by Dana Placeholder. Default by the subtenant does not release Acme Example Co from its own duties to the master landlord.
