---
title: "Cedar Plaza Retail Lease"
type: note
---

This lease covers Unit 14, a 1,850 square foot storefront at Cedar Plaza, rented by Acme Example Co from Willow Ridge Holdings Example for a demonstration showroom. The term is five years from August 1, 2022. Base rent is $4,900 monthly plus percentage rent of five percent of gross sales above a natural breakpoint of $1,176,000 per year. Tenant pays common area maintenance, a marketing fund contribution of $350 monthly, and its share of taxes. Operating hours must match the center's schedule of ten to eight on weekdays. Signage must follow the center's sign criteria and receive advance approval. The lease includes a co-tenancy clause: if the anchor grocer closes for more than 120 days, rent drops to fifty percent until a replacement opens. Radius restriction is three miles. Guarantor is not required. Contact for the landlord is Henry Sample, property manager. Requests for confirmation of lease status by lenders are answered through a separate certificate signed by the tenant.
