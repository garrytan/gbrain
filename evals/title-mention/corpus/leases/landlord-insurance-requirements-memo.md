---
title: "Landlord Insurance Requirements Memo"
type: note
---

This memo summarizes the insurance coverage that landlords require of Acme Example Co across its leased locations, prepared by Ivan Placeholder in Risk Management. Commercial general liability must be at least $2 million per occurrence and $4 million aggregate at every site. The Harbor Street and Maple Court landlords also require an umbrella policy of $5 million. The Riverside warehouse landlord requires property coverage on inventory and racking, with a maximum deductible of $25,000. Cedar Plaza requires plate glass coverage and business interruption insurance for twelve months. Each landlord must be named as additional insured, and certificates are due before commencement and at every policy renewal. Cancellation notice of thirty days must be provided to landlords. Subtenants must carry equivalent liability coverage. The memo lists renewal dates, brokers, and premium allocations by location. Questions about certificate requests go to the risk team, and missing certificates may trigger default notices.
