---
title: "Riverside Warehouse Lease Renewal"
type: note
---

This renewal extends the lease of the Riverside distribution facility at 900 Dock Road, comprising 32,000 square feet of warehouse space and 2,400 square feet of attached office. Acme Example Co exercised its renewal option on April 15, 2024, and the landlord, Tidewater Realty Example, accepted in writing. The new term is five years, from January 1, 2025 through December 31, 2029. Starting monthly rent is $21,333, reset to market under the option clause, with annual increases set out in a separate schedule attached as Exhibit B. The security deposit of $42,666 carries over unchanged. The landlord will replace two dock levelers and repair the roof membrane by June 30, 2025, at its own cost. The tenant keeps responsibility for forklift charging areas and interior racking. A one-time right to terminate after month 36 is available with 180 days notice and payment of unamortized concessions. Frank Example signed for the tenant.
