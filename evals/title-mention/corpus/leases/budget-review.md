title: "Budget Review"
type: note
---

This session examined occupancy costs for the fiscal year across all leased properties. Total base rent is forecast at $1.42 million, with Harbor Street rising after the new term began and the Dock Road warehouse increasing under the fixed annual schedule. Sublease income from the Maple Court subtenant offsets about $78,000 of rent. Operating expense pass-throughs are expected to exceed the plan by four percent, driven by snow removal and higher property taxes; Grace Placeholder recommended a reserve of $35,000. Insurance premiums rose eleven percent, mostly from higher umbrella coverage requirements by landlords. The improvement allowance from the Harbor Street landlord is treated as a receivable until paid. Retail costs at Cedar Plaza include the marketing fund contribution and possible percentage rent, which is currently not triggered. Recommendations: negotiate cap language on controllable expenses in future renewals, audit common area charges at two sites, and evaluate consolidating storage space before the next expiration.
