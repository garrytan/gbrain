---
title: "Harbor Street Lease Agreement"
type: note
---

This document records the original commercial lease between Acme Example Co, as tenant, and Northgate Holdings Example, as landlord, for the second and third floors of 41 Harbor Street. The term runs five years from March 1, 2021, with base rent of $18,500 per month, payable on the first business day. The tenant deposits a security amount equal to two months of rent. Permitted use is general office space; any change requires written landlord consent. The tenant pays a proportionate share of operating expenses and property taxes, estimated at 14.2 percent of the building. Two parking spaces are included at no additional charge. The tenant holds one option to renew for three years at ninety-five percent of prevailing market rent, exercised by written notice at least nine months before expiry. Early termination is not permitted except after casualty or condemnation. Notices go to Alice Example, Real Estate Manager. Later changes to rent, term, or parking are handled by separate written modifications rather than by editing this original.
