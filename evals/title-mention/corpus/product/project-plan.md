Goal: ship the first Lantern app release to both stores by the end of the quarter. Phase one, weeks 1-3: finalize requirements with Frank Placeholder and lock the alert payload format with the hub team. Phase two, weeks 4-8: build the alert feed, floor map, and offline cache; Grace Example leads a team of four engineers. Phase three, weeks 9-10: accessibility audit, performance tuning, and a beta with 20 warehouse staff. Phase four, weeks 11-12: store submission and staged rollout. Dependencies: hub firmware exposing per-device alerts, a single sign-on test tenant, and design assets from Olivia Sample. Risks: store review delays and late changes to the alert format. Weekly demos are held on Thursdays. Exit criteria: crash-free rate above 99.5 percent during the beta period, and no open accessibility defects.
