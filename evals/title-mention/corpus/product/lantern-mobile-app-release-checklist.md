---
title: "Lantern Mobile App Release Checklist"
type: note
---

Steps to complete before each Lantern app release goes to the stores. 1. Freeze the release branch and confirm all requirement tickets are closed or deferred. 2. Run the full automated suite on three reference devices, including one low-memory phone. 3. Verify push alerts arrive within 10 seconds against the staging hub. 4. Check that cold start is under 2.5 seconds. 5. Confirm the accessibility audit passes with large text and a screen reader. 6. Update the version number, changelog, and privacy disclosure. 7. Get sign-off from Frank Placeholder and Grace Example. 8. Submit for store review at least five business days before the planned date. 9. Stage the rollout at 5, 25, then 100 percent, watching that the crash-free rate stays above 99.5 percent. 10. Archive build artifacts and symbols. Rollback owner: Henry Sample.
