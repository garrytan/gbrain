---
title: "Lantern Mobile App Requirements"
type: note
---

Product requirements for the Lantern phone application, which lets warehouse staff view sensor alerts and dock status. It must support the two latest major versions of both mobile operating systems. Core features: push alerts within 10 seconds of a hub event, a floor map showing sensor locations, acknowledgement of an alert with a single tap, and offline viewing of the last 24 hours of readings. Login uses company single sign-on with optional biometric unlock. Accessibility: all screens must support large text and screen readers. Performance: cold start under 2.5 seconds on a mid-range device. Out of scope for the first release: editing hub settings and dark mode. Product owner: Frank Placeholder. Engineering lead: Grace Example. Open question: whether alerts from the newer hub should display per-device battery levels alongside the sensor name and floor location.
