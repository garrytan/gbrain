---
title: "Quartz Display Panel Test Plan"
type: note
---

Test approach for the 7-inch Quartz screen used on the dock and hub fronts. Incoming inspection samples 32 panels from each lot of 1,000. Tests: brightness uniformity above 85 percent across nine points, minimum brightness of 450 nits, a dead pixel limit of zero in the central zone and two elsewhere, touch accuracy within 1.5 mm, and a 500-hour burn-in at 50 C. Environmental tests: thermal shock between -10 and 60 C for 50 cycles, and exposure to 85 percent humidity for 96 hours. A failure rate above 3 percent triggers lot rejection and notice to the supplier contact, Ivy Placeholder. Testing takes place in the Harbor Street lab. Owner: Jack Example. Color tuning for panels that pass is handled in a separate calibration procedure.
