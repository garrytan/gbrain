---
title: "Widget Packaging Redesign Proposal"
type: note
---

Proposal to shrink and simplify retail packaging for the dock and hub. The current carton measures 320 by 240 by 110 mm and uses molded foam inserts. The new design uses a 260 by 200 by 95 mm carton with recycled pulp trays, cutting volume by about 32 percent and reducing pallet count per shipment by one fifth. Drop tests from 1.0 meter on all six faces must pass with no cosmetic damage. Estimated unit cost falls by 0.42 dollars, with a one-time tooling charge of 18,000 dollars that is paid back in about seven months at forecast volume. The printed quick-start guide is replaced with a QR code plus a single fold card. Proposed by Liam Example, pending review by Maya Placeholder. A decision is needed before the second-quarter pilot build so that tooling can be ordered in time.
