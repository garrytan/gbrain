---
title: "Quartz Display Panel Calibration Guide"
type: note
---

Procedure for calibrating each Quartz screen after it passes incoming inspection. Warm the panel for 20 minutes at full brightness. Place the colorimeter at the center point and measure white, red, green, blue, and five gray levels. The target white point is 6500 K with delta-E below 3. Write the 256-entry gamma table to the panel controller through the service cable, then remeasure at nine points. If uniformity falls under 85 percent, apply the compensation map and repeat once; a second failure sends the unit to rework. Touch calibration uses a five-point grid with 1.5 mm tolerance. Record the serial number, results, and operator in the production log. The fixture is maintained by Jack Example, and the colorimeter is recalibrated every six months by Kara Sample. Sampling, burn-in, and environmental tests for incoming lots are covered elsewhere.
