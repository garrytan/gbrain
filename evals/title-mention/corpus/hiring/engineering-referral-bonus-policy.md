---
title: "Engineering Referral Bonus Policy"
type: note
---

Employees who refer a candidate hired into an engineering position earn a referral bonus. The amount is 2,500 for standard positions and 4,000 for senior positions, such as backend openings at level L5 or above. Payment is split: half is paid after the new hire's start date and half after ninety days of employment. The referrer must submit the candidate through the referral tracker before the first recruiter screen; later submissions do not qualify. Managers in the same reporting chain as the hire are not eligible, and recruiters cannot claim bonuses. Contractors who later convert to employees are not eligible for referral payouts on their own conversion, though the person who originally referred them may qualify. Bonuses are paid with the next monthly payroll after each milestone. Questions go to Erin Demo. The policy is reviewed each January, and any change to amounts is announced to all engineering staff by email.
