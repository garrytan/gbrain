This amendment revises the support headcount numbers approved earlier in the fiscal year. Changes: the tier-two specialist hire moves up from June to April; the team lead requisition is cancelled and replaced with an internal promotion; and tier-one agent hires increase from four to five because of the new weekend shift. Training cohort seats rise from four to five per quarter. Incremental cost changes from 412,000 to 431,500 including benefits. Carol Placeholder requested the changes after the ticket volume review, and Dan Sample approved them. Everything not mentioned here, including salary assumptions, the 4 percent merit adjustment, and attrition backfills at 10 percent, remains as written in the original support headcount document. For the items listed above, the original figures are superseded by this revision. Recruiting should reopen the agent requisitions immediately and move the specialist posting to the front of the queue.
