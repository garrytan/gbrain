---
title: "Harbor Street Recruiter Job Description"
type: note
---

Acme Example Co is seeking a full-time technical recruiter for the Harbor Street office. The recruiter partners with hiring managers, writes postings, screens candidates, and coordinates interview loops. Requirements: three years of in-house or agency recruiting, comfort with applicant tracking systems, and clear written communication. The salary band is 72,000 to 88,000, plus a placement bonus paid after a hire completes ninety days. The recruiter reports to Erin Demo, Head of People. Core metrics are time to offer, offer acceptance rate, and candidate satisfaction survey score. First-quarter priorities include the backend engineer and data analyst openings and cleaning up stale requisitions in the tracking system. The recruiter also maintains the referral tracker and schedules quarterly reviews of each department's hiring plan with its lead. Occasional travel to meetups and campus events is expected, and the position includes two remote days per week.
