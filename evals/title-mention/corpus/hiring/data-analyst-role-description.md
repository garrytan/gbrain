title: "Data Analyst Role Description"
type: note
---

The Data Analyst position supports the Finance and Operations group at Acme Example Co and reports to Frank Illustrative, Director of Operations. The analyst builds weekly dashboards, maintains revenue and support-volume reports, and answers ad hoc questions from department leads. Required experience: two years of analytics work, advanced spreadsheet skills, and fluent SQL. Preferred experience: a scripting language and a dashboard tool. The level is L3, with a salary band of 68,000 to 82,000. The position is based at the Harbor Street office with hybrid scheduling. Responsibilities include documenting data definitions, checking report accuracy, and presenting findings monthly. Success at ninety days means taking over the weekly operations dashboard and closing the open data quality tickets. Candidate evaluation criteria and the scoring scale live in a separate scorecard. Openings and timelines are handled by the recruiter, and this page covers duties and qualifications only.
