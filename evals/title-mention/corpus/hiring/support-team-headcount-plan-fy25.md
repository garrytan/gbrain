This document sets headcount for the customer support organization in fiscal 2025. The current team is 14 agents across two shifts under Carol Placeholder. Total incremental cost is 412,000 including benefits. Backfills for attrition are handled separately and assumed at 10 percent per year. Dan Sample signed the approval during the original budget cycle. Any later changes to wave timing or counts are recorded in a follow-up document rather than edited here, so this page reflects the first approved numbers.
