---
title: "Contractor Conversion Guidelines"
type: note
---

These guidelines explain how a contractor at Acme Example Co can move onto payroll as a regular employee. A contractor becomes eligible after six months of continuous engagement and a written recommendation from the sponsoring manager. The manager must confirm that an approved headcount slot exists in the department's hiring plan; conversions cannot create new headcount. The recruiter runs a shortened interview loop of two conversations rather than the full process. Compensation is set on the internal ladder for the level of the work performed, not the contractor's previous hourly rate. A conversion fee is not paid to any agency after twelve months of engagement. Employment starts on the first day of a pay period, and benefits begin the following month. Erin Demo approves every conversion, and Grace Sample in Finance updates the headcount records. Managers should start the process at least 45 days before the contract end date.
