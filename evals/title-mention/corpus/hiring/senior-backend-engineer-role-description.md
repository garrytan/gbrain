title: "Senior Backend Engineer Role Description"
type: note
---

The Senior Backend Engineer position sits in the Platform group at Acme Example Co and reports to Bob Sample, Engineering Manager. The engineer owns the order-processing services, designs internal APIs, and leads code reviews for two junior teammates. Required experience: six years building distributed services, strong SQL skills, and prior on-call ownership. Preferred experience: message queues and payment integrations. The level is L5, with a salary band of 128,000 to 152,000 on the internal ladder. The position is based at the Harbor Street office with two remote days per week. Responsibilities also include capacity planning, incident retrospectives, and mentoring. Success at ninety days means shipping one service improvement and joining the on-call rotation. The interview timeline, sourcing channels, and number of openings are tracked separately in the hiring plan for this position. This page covers duties, level, and qualifications only.
