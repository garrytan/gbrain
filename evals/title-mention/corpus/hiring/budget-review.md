Support hiring shows a variance: the amended headcount raises incremental cost by 19,500. Dan Sample and Grace Sample recommend covering that variance from unused contractor conversion funds. Open questions include whether to renew the applicant tracking license at a higher tier and whether to prepay agency retainers. The final numbers will be presented to Erin Demo at month end.
