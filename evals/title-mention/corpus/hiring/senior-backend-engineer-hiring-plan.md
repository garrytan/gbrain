title: "Senior Backend Engineer Hiring Plan"
type: note
---

This plan covers filling two Senior Backend Engineer openings in the Platform group during the second half of the year. Recruiter Alice Example opens both requisitions on the first of July. The interview stages are a recruiter screen, a take-home exercise, a system design interview, and a values conversation with Bob Sample. The target time to offer is 35 days. Sourcing channels are employee referrals, two engineering meetups, and an agency shortlist capped at ten candidates. The budget is 8,000 for agency fees and 3,000 for meetup sponsorship. Target start dates are mid-September and mid-October. Duties, level, salary band, and qualifications are documented in the position's role description, not here. Known risks include a competing offer cycle in August and limited interviewer availability during quarter close. Pipeline counts go to the hiring manager every Friday until both offers are accepted.
