---
title: "Data Analyst Interview Rubric"
type: note
---

This scorecard is used by every interviewer who meets a Data Analyst candidate at Acme Example Co. Each of four areas is scored from 1 to 4: SQL fluency, analytical reasoning, communication of findings, and collaboration with non-technical partners. The SQL round uses a live exercise with a messy orders table. The reasoning round asks the candidate to diagnose a sudden drop in weekly support volume. In the communication round, the candidate presents a two-slide summary to Frank Illustrative. Scores below 2 in any area are a no-hire unless the panel documents an exception. A total of 12 or higher moves the candidate to the offer discussion. Interviewers submit written feedback within 24 hours and must not read other scores before submitting their own. The recruiter compiles the results. The duties, level, and salary band for the position are described in its separate posting.
