---
title: "Offsite Dietary Catering Menu"
type: note
---

This document lists the meals and dietary accommodations ordered for the spring offsite at Harbor Street. Catering comes from Fictional Fork Catering, coordinated by Erin Mockwell. Based on registration forms, the order includes 14 vegetarian meals, 6 vegan meals, 5 gluten-free meals, and 3 meals prepared without nuts for severe allergies. Day one lunch is a build-your-own grain bowl bar, and dinner is a choice of roasted chicken, mushroom risotto, or baked salmon. Day two lunch is sandwiches and salads. Coffee, tea, and snacks are refreshed at 10:00 and 15:00 each day. Allergy-safe meals will be labeled with attendee names and served from a separate table. Anyone whose needs changed after registration should email the events team by 5 May. The catering fee of $6,300 is separate from the venue rental and is paid on delivery.
