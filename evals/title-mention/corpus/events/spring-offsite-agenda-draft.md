This is the first working schedule for the spring offsite, prepared by Alice Example and circulated for comments on 3 March. Day one, 14 May at Harbor Street: registration at 9:00, opening keynote at 9:30, four parallel breakout sessions from 11:00, lunch at 12:30, a strategy workshop at 14:00, and a group dinner at 18:00. Day two, 15 May: a morning hike along the waterfront trail, a cross-team retrospective at 11:00, lunch, and a closing circle that ends at 16:00. Several items are still open, including who will lead the fourth breakout and whether the hike is realistic in spring weather. Department leads should send feedback to Alice Example by 10 March. Nothing in this schedule is approved, and times may shift once the venue and shuttle details are confirmed. Attendees should not book travel around these times yet.
