This approved schedule replaces the earlier working version of the spring offsite plan and reflects all feedback gathered in March. Day one, 14 May at Harbor Street: registration at 9:00, the keynote moved to 10:00, three breakout sessions instead of four, lunch at 12:30, the strategy workshop at 14:00, and dinner at 18:30. Day two, 15 May: the waterfront hike was cancelled because of the rain forecast and replaced with an indoor design workshop led by Bob Sample. The retrospective stays at 11:00, and the closing circle now ends at 15:00 so shuttles can leave earlier. Alice Example signed off on the final version on 24 March. Any further changes must go through the events committee. Attendees can now book travel around these times, and room assignments will follow in a separate message from the events team.
