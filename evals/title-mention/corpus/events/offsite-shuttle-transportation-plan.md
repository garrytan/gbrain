---
title: "Offsite Shuttle Transportation Plan"
type: note
---

This plan describes how attendees will travel between the main office and Harbor Street for the spring offsite. Dan Testcase is coordinating three shuttles, each with 25 seats. Departures from the office lot are at 7:30, 8:00, and 8:15 on 14 May, and return trips leave the venue at 16:30 on 15 May after the closing circle. Attendees are assigned to a shuttle by department, and the list is posted on the events page. Parking at the venue is limited to 20 vehicles, so only people with mobility needs or early departures may drive; they should register with Dan Testcase by 30 April. A luggage trailer will follow the third shuttle. The driver will wait ten minutes for latecomers only. Shuttle costs of $2,100 are billed separately from the rental fee and charged to the events budget.
