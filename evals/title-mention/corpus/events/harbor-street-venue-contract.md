title: "Harbor Street Venue Contract"
type: note
---

This agreement covers the rental of the Harbor Street Conference Hall for the spring offsite on 14 and 15 May. It was signed by Bob Sample for Acme Example Co. The hall is reserved for up to 80 guests at a total fee of $18,400. A 30 percent deposit is due by 1 April, with the balance payable seven days before the event. Audio and video equipment, tables, and on-site parking for 20 vehicles are included. Catering is not included and must be arranged separately. Cancellation requires 21 days written notice for a full refund of the deposit; later cancellations forfeit it. Setup access begins at 7:00 on the first day, and the hall must be cleared by 17:00 on the second day. Any damage is billed to Acme Example Co. Questions about terms go to the venue manager, Grace Placeholder.
