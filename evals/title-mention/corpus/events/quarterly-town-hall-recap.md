title: "Quarterly Town Hall Recap"
type: note
---

This summary covers the second-quarter all-hands held on 11 June in the main auditorium and streamed to remote offices. Alice Example opened with company results, followed by a product roadmap presentation. On events, the committee announced that the summer picnic will take place on 20 July and that volunteers are needed. It also confirmed that planning for the December holiday party has started, with a budget approved in principle. Employees asked whether the next offsite could include partners for one dinner; the committee agreed to study the cost. Other questions covered remote travel stipends and parking near the office. Attendance was 212 in person and 148 online. The recording is available on the internal video page for 90 days. A short feedback survey remains open until 25 June, and results will be shared at the next gathering.
