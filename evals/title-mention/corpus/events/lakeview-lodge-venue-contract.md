title: "Lakeview Lodge Venue Contract"
type: note
---

This agreement covers a three-day, two-night stay at Lakeview Lodge for the autumn leadership retreat, 9 to 11 October. It was signed by Carol Placeholder for Acme Example Co. The lodge is booked for 45 guests at a total of $22,750, which includes lodging, all meals, and use of the lakeside meeting room. Audio and video equipment is not included and must be rented separately. A 50 percent deposit is due by 1 August, with the balance due 30 days before arrival. Cancellation needs 45 days written notice for a deposit refund. Parking is free but limited to 15 vehicles, so carpooling is expected. This was the alternative considered for the spring gathering but was judged too costly and too remote for a group of eighty. Contact the lodge coordinator, Henry Sample, with rooming questions.
