---
title: "Annual Holiday Party Budget"
type: note
---

This document sets out the approved spending plan for the December holiday party, owned by Erin Mockwell. The total budget is $12,000 for roughly 90 guests. Venue rental at the Riverside Ballroom accounts for $4,500. Catering and beverages are $4,200, live music is $900, decorations are $600, employee gift cards are $1,200, and a contingency reserve is $600. The plan assumes a per-head catering cost of about $47, and any headcount above 95 requires a budget amendment. Invoices must be submitted to finance within ten days of the event. Last year the party ran about eight percent over plan because of late additions, so the contingency has been raised. Erin Mockwell will send a monthly spending summary to the events committee starting in September. Vendors are being chosen from quotes gathered in June.
