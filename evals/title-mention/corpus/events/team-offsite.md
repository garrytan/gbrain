This overview introduces the spring gathering for Acme Example Co, held 14 and 15 May at Harbor Street. The purpose is to align teams on next year's priorities, build connections across departments, and reflect on recent projects. All full-time employees are invited, and managers should ensure coverage for urgent customer requests. Expect a mix of talks, workshops, and social time, with casual dress recommended. Bring a laptop, a reusable water bottle, and comfortable shoes. Shuttles leave from the main office, and meals are provided with dietary options. Rooms are not needed since everyone returns home each evening. Questions should go to Alice Example, who chairs the events committee. Detailed pages about the schedule, venue terms, transport, and catering are linked from the events section of the intranet. Please complete the registration form by 30 April so headcounts are accurate.
