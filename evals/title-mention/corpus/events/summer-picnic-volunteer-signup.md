---
title: "Summer Picnic Volunteer Signup"
type: note
---

Acme Example Co will hold its family picnic on 20 July at Maple Field, and the events committee needs about 24 volunteers. Roles include the setup crew, who arrive at 9:00 to place tents and tables; the grill team, who cook from 11:30 to 14:00; the children's games station; and the cleanup crew, who stay until 17:00. Each volunteer picks one shift of about three hours and receives a free lunch and an extra half day of paid time off. Sign-ups close on 30 June, and Frank Dummy will confirm assignments by email the following week. Volunteers should wear the orange event shirt, which will be delivered to each office. If you have food safety certification, please mention it, since the grill team needs at least two certified members. Rain plans will be announced 48 hours ahead.
