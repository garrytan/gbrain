---
title: "Q3 Engineering Budget Variance"
type: note
---

The engineering department finished the third quarter slightly over budget. The approved budget was $1,150,000 and actual spend was $1,213,000, an overspend of $63,000, or 5.5%. Cloud hosting was $88,000 over plan because of load testing for the new release. Contractor spend was $34,000 over after two short-term hires covered a sprint. These overruns were partly offset by $59,000 of savings from delayed permanent hiring. Henry Placeholder owns the department budget and has committed to reducing hosting costs by moving test environments to scheduled shutdowns. Finance will monitor monthly hosting invoices and review the run rate at the next close. The marketing and other departmental variances are reported separately, and this report covers engineering only. No transfer of funds from other departments is requested for the fourth quarter.
