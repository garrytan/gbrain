---
title: "Q3 Marketing Budget Variance"
type: note
---

The marketing department finished the third quarter over budget. The approved budget was $420,000 and actual spend was $468,000, an overspend of $48,000, or 11.4%. The main driver was the Lakeview Expo trade show, which cost $39,000 more than planned after booth upgrades and late shipping fees. Paid search spend was $9,000 over because of a promotion tied to the widget price change. Content production came in $6,000 under budget after a freelancer contract ended early. Grace Sample owns the department budget and explained the variance to finance. She proposes shifting $25,000 from the fourth-quarter brand campaign to cover part of the overrun. Finance asks for revised event estimates before approving any transfer. The engineering and other departmental variances are reported separately, and this report covers marketing only.
