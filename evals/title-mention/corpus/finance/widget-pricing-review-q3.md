---
title: "Widget Pricing Review Q3"
type: note
---

This paper documents the third-quarter pricing decision for the widget product line, approved by Erin Placeholder. The list price of the Standard Widget rises from $48 to $52, and the Deluxe Widget rises from $85 to $91. Volume discount tiers are tightened so that orders above 500 units receive 6% instead of 9%. Finance modeled price elasticity using the last four quarters of order data and expects unit volume to fall about 3%, while revenue improves by roughly $310,000 for the quarter. Customer notices go out 30 days before the effective date. The analysis compares results against the earlier second-quarter changes and flags the enterprise segment as the most sensitive. Gross margin on the Standard Widget is projected to reach 41%. Results will be checked again at the end of the fiscal year.
