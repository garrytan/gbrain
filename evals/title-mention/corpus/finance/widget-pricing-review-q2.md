---
title: "Widget Pricing Review Q2"
type: note
---

This paper documents the second-quarter pricing decision for the widget product line, prepared by Frank Example. The list price of the Standard Widget rises modestly from $46 to $48, while the Deluxe Widget stays at $85. Finance benchmarked prices against three competitors and found Acme Example Co about 5% below the median. Volume discount tiers are unchanged: orders above 500 units receive 9%. Price elasticity modeling suggests unit volume falls under 1%, with revenue improving by roughly $120,000 for the quarter. Customer notices go out 45 days before the effective date. Gross margin on the Standard Widget is projected at 38%. The review recommends revisiting Deluxe pricing in the next quarter if input costs continue to rise. Later pricing changes build on these figures and should be compared against them when measuring results.
