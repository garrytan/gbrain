---
title: "Capital Equipment Purchase Forecast"
type: note
---

This forecast lists planned capital purchases for the fiscal year, totaling $640,000. The largest item is a new packaging line at $310,000 for the Riverside Road warehouse. Also planned are two forklifts at $85,000, replacement laptops for the engineering team at $120,000, and a hardware test bench at $125,000. Jack Sample prepared the forecast and recommends deferring the test bench to the next fiscal year if cash flow tightens. All items are depreciated straight-line over five years, which adds about $128,000 per year to depreciation expense once everything is in service. Purchases above $100,000 need approval from the finance director. The forecast assumes vendor quotes remain valid through the third quarter and includes a 5% contingency on the packaging line. Cash timing is reviewed monthly.
