---
title: "Travel Expense Policy Update"
type: note
---

Effective October 1, Acme Example Co is revising its rules for employee travel reimbursement. Daily meal per diem is capped at $75 for domestic trips and $110 for international trips. Hotel stays are capped at $210 per night unless a manager approves an exception. Airfare must be booked at least 14 days in advance, and receipts are required for any expense above $25. Ivy Example in finance will approve exceptions and review monthly reports of spend by department. The revision replaces the older flat allowance, which caused inconsistent claims and several disputes last year. Finance estimates annual savings of about $54,000, mostly from advance airfare booking and hotel caps. Departments that exceed their travel budget by more than 10% in a quarter must submit an explanation. Questions can be sent to the finance help desk.
