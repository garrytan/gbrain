---
title: "Harbor Street Lease Agreement"
type: note
---

This summary covers the original office lease for the Harbor Street headquarters, signed by Alice Example for Acme Example Co and Bob Sample for the landlord. The term is five years, covering floors one and two, a total of 18,000 square feet. Monthly base rent starts at $41,000 with a 3% annual escalation. The security deposit is $82,000. The tenant may terminate early after month 36 by giving six months of written notice and paying a fee equal to three months of rent. The tenant improvement allowance is $40,000. Parking is billed separately at $150 per space each month. Finance books rent as a fixed monthly expense in the facilities budget line and reviews occupancy accruals every quarter. Later changes to rent, space, or term are handled in separate documents and are not reflected in these original figures.
