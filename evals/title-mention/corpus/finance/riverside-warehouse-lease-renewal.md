---
title: "Riverside Warehouse Lease Renewal"
type: note
---

Acme Example Co has agreed to renew the lease on the Riverside Road warehouse that stores widget inventory. The landlord, Dana Sample, offered a three-year renewal at 30,000 square feet. Monthly rent moves from $22,000 to $23,400, and common area maintenance charges are capped at 4% growth per year. Finance compared this with relocating to a new site and found that moving costs of about $210,000 outweighed the rent savings. The renewal removes the option to sublease part of the space. Frank Example will update the fixed cost forecast for the fourth quarter, and the added rent of roughly $4,200 per quarter will be charged to the operations budget. Signing is expected once legal confirms the insurance certificate. This lease is separate from the headquarters office lease and has different renewal dates and terms.
