Fixed: `gbrain access member handover --yes --expect <hash>` now applies a preview only for the member it was made for, and never rewrites a folder rule that moved to a new prefix after the preview. Either mismatch refuses with `preview_changed` and writes nothing. Before this fix, an apply naming a different member, or resuming after a rule moved, could carry out the other preview. A handover previewed on 0.60.747.0 needs to be previewed again before it is applied.
