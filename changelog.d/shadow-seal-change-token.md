**A shadow follower seal carried to the next `gbrain serve` session now rests on the database's own write counters, not on the writer records.**

### Fixed
- The check that lets a short serve session carry the shadow coverage watermark over to the next one compared writer-session rows. A writer whose row was refreshed but was not the newest, a role with no way to record its writes, or a write made while both evidence tables were locked left those rows unchanged, so the next session trusted a seal it should not have. The seal now holds a versioned token of the server's statistics epoch and, for each relation the seed copied, its identity and insert, update and delete counts; the next session confirms it only when all of that is unchanged. It also refuses while another connection has a write open, a prepared transaction exists, a recorded writer is still connected, another connection of a role that can write a copied table is not idle, went idle in the last minute or cannot be seen by the reading role, or a login role can write without a way to record it. A read-only connection, such as a monitoring agent, is never waited on. It refuses on `track_counts` off and on PostgreSQL older than 15, and withdraws a confirmation if a write opens right after it. Each refusal is named in `engine shadow status`. A writer still connected during the seal check is refused until it commits or disconnects, so a session that closes while another client is connected may need a full pass.
