**Refreshing shared skills clears a moved policy epoch, old slugs show page history after a merge, free conversation relinks use the page's own links, and the local reranker guide sets its batch sizes.**

### Fixed
- `gbrain bootstrap harness --refresh-skills` now clears `requirements_changed` after a source's skill policy moves its epoch. An approved `join_brain` on an active enrollment renews the pin of each source it already follows when the policy asks for no new tool, file class or audience, and moves the enrollment epoch. A new tool, class or audience, a new source, a recreated source, or an enrollment made before this release still needs the owner's approval of a new enrollment. On PostgreSQL the batch a narrowing or renewing join issues can now be acknowledged; before, its acknowledgment always read as historical. Reported by @benswinney (#6797). Contributed by @benswinney (#10585) and @javieraldape (#9409).
- `get_versions` on an old slug that a merge or rename alias points at, with no page left at that slug, now lists the surviving page's history, as `get_page` opens it. The alias is read in the caller's scope and privacy, a page that holds the slug keeps its own history, and readers and `visibility: private` apply to the surviving page, so an old slug never shows more than the surviving page's own slug. Reported by @liuwenyong1985 (#10586). Contributed by @Masashi-Ono0611 (#10587).
- `gbrain facts relink --conversation` now links a fact that names a participant by first name when the conversation page links that person. Among the people the page itself links, a first name names one person. A fact that names two of them, or another person who shares the first name, stays unlinked, and a project or company is matched only by its full title. A page the relink already tried is picked again once it gains a link its author wrote, so links added after extraction no longer need a paid re-extraction. Reported by @speech115 (#10588). Contributed by @Masashi-Ono0611 (#10590).

### Docs
- The local llama-server reranker guide and setup hint launch the server with `-b 2048 -ub 2048`, explain the HTTP 500 a 512-token physical batch gives on longer documents, and add a long-document check, since the doctor probe sends only short passages. Reported by @HirotakeMachiyama (#10614). Contributed by @Masashi-Ono0611 (#10615).
