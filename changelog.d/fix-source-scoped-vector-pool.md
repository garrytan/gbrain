**A source-scoped vector search whose scope scan times out now falls back to the HNSW index walk instead of a full-corpus scan.** A source holding at most 25k chunks and under 30% of pages runs the exact scope scan first. Until now that scope had no index walk at all, so when the scan hit its 2 s attempt budget (cold pages, a busy disk) the only next statement was the joined pool statement. On a cold brain the planner runs that as a sequential scan and distance sort of every chunk, which ran into the 8 s vector deadline and left the vector arm empty, reported as `vector_candidates_incomplete` (`timeout`). The scope now keeps the walk as the scan's fallback, so the order is scope scan, then walk, then the joined statement. A scan that answers is unchanged and still exact, and the walk applies every source, visibility and page filter, as it already does for wider scopes. If the walk also times out or comes back short, the joined statement runs as it does behind any walk, with materialization off (`JOINED_STATEMENT_SETTINGS`).

The walk fallback covers scopes the walk already serves: at least 4% of pages (`INDEX_WALK_MIN_SCOPE_SHARE`), a relaxed search on the HNSW-indexed `embedding` column, and no type or date filter. A smaller or narrowed scope still goes from a timed-out scan to the joined statement. Each attempt keeps its own Postgres `statement_timeout` (2 s for the scan and each walk window, the remaining vector budget for the joined statement); there is no new end-to-end deadline across them, and PGLite attempts are not cancelled.

### Itemized changes

- `src/core/search/vector-statement.ts`: a scan-first scope keeps `indexWalkSql` and sets the new `scopeScanFirst` flag; the scan SQL, the walk SQL and the routing thresholds are unchanged.
- `src/core/search/vector-pool.ts`: `searchIndexWalk` runs the scope scan before the walk when `scopeScanFirst` is set, and never runs the scan twice.
- `src/core/postgres-engine.ts`, `src/core/pglite-engine.ts`: `explainVectorSearch` (doctor `vector_plan`) still explains the scan for a scan-first scope; the joined statement behind a scan-first scope's walk now gets `JOINED_STATEMENT_SETTINGS` like every other statement with a walk.
- Docs: `docs/architecture/key-files/core-search-2.md`.
- Tests: `test/search/vector-pool.test.ts`, `test/search/vector-statement.test.ts`, `test/search/vector-index-walk.test.ts` (PGLite: a timed-out scan falls to the walk, not the pool, and returns the joined statement's rows; when the scan and the walk both time out, the joined statement runs with materialization off), `test/search/vector-scope-chunks.test.ts`, and `test/e2e/vector-scope-recall-postgres.test.ts` (Postgres, 1024-dim: walk after a timed-out scan, recall against the exact statement).
