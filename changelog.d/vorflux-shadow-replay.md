**You can record every call your brain makes to its storage engine and replay it each night on private PGLite, Rust and optional local Postgres copies, to find where the Rust engine answers differently.**

### Added
- `gbrain engine shadow capture on --yes | off | status | delete --yes`. Capture is off by default. When on, every engine call (reads and writes, with their arguments) is recorded in owner-only files under `~/.gbrain/shadow/<brain>/capture/`; results are kept only as hashes. `capture off` or `GBRAIN_SHADOW_CAPTURE=off` stops it at the next write and deletes nothing. Any new purge-log line deletes the whole current recording and its copies, and drops the local Postgres scratch copy, in the process that purged, even with capture off.
- `gbrain engine shadow replay` replays the recording on copies seeded from one snapshot of the brain, in a child process with a throwaway home, no API keys and no network, compares the copies with each other, reads back every page a write named, and writes a local report plus a summary that holds only operation, field path and keyed hash. Guide: `docs/guides/shadow-replay.md`.
