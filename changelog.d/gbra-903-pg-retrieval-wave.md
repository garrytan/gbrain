**Keyword search on a small Postgres brain stops timing out behind the write bookkeeping's own maintenance reads, date-filtered vector search on large brains gets faster and more complete, and paging through Chinese, Japanese or Korean keyword results no longer repeats or skips pages.**

### Fixed
- The segmented-storage gate's evidence read (doctor and the maintenance tick) scanned the whole write-receipt table on each call, about 270 MB of reads at 200,000 receipts in the report. On a small managed database that pushed the search data out of the cache, so hybrid search's keyword arm kept timing out. Migration `persistence_request_window_indexes` adds an index on each receipt's admission time and one on the publication time of committed receipts, built online on Postgres and inline on PGLite, and the read now touches only the receipts inside its window. `gbrain repair request-indexes` rebuilds either index. Part of #10605. Reported by @thebergerking91.
- Doctor's `keyword_plan` warning on a brain under 500,000 chunks now says a slow keyword statement is more often waiting on disk reads than ranking matches, names `pg_stat_statements` ordered by `shared_blks_read` as the way to find what evicts the search data, and sets `cache_contention_suspected` in its details. The troubleshooting guide has a new section for this case, including how to give back the disk space compacted receipts leave behind (`pg_repack`, or `VACUUM FULL` with writers stopped).
- Paging through CJK keyword results with the OR fallback (`limit` and `offset`) could repeat a page or skip one. A long page with many matching chunks filled the candidate pool before results were grouped by page, which pushed shorter pages past a later offset, and the strict pass and its any-term retry ordered pages differently with OR alternatives. Page-grain CJK search now keeps the best chunk of each page before cutting the page window, and a multi-term strict pass ranks pages exactly as the retry does, so every offset page is a slice of one fixed order. Display columns are read for the returned rows only.

### Changed
- On a Postgres brain of at least 375,000 chunks (the planner's estimate, read at most once a minute and only for date-bounded searches), a vector search with a date bound, alone or with a type filter, runs with materialization off. On 1% date filters this returned all of the true top 20 pages instead of 60% to 87%, with no measured slowdown (gbrain-evals#671). Smaller brains, frontmatter filters, slug or slug-prefix exclusions, language, symbol-kind and low-detail filters, and narrow source scopes keep today's plan, and the sort setting and exact-fallback routing of searches that use the index walk are unchanged. Fixes #9817.
